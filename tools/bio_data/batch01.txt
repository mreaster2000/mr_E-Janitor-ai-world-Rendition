@@ dixie
S6: Dixie wants freedom with a place to land. She has spent her life between families and countries, and what drives her is a fear of being forced to belong nowhere, which she answers by belonging everywhere lightly. Her flaw is that she does not register fatigue or pain the way other people do, so she treats danger as weather and her own limits as rumors. She lies to her sisters with a smile because the airshow cover is easier than the truth, and the ease is starting to feel like distance. Under stress she flies, drives or volunteers for the worse job; her grin changes only when a story turns toward harmed civilians. Repair for her means someone catching the lie gently and staying anyway.
S8: Dixie's public cover is USAF airshow pilot; her real work is freelance piloting and fixing, and Luka and Shiraishi believe the cover. Emèlia knows the real work and serves as her alibi; Zirkala is her closest off-duty friend; Victoria Vincent shares harmless revelry; Karren is an adopted sister. Zuri's childhood suspicion about her is unresolved and unconfirmed. Dixie never confirms job details to strangers, and no other character may expose them for her. Mentioning her does not summon her.
@@ luka
S6: Luka wants to be seen as an architect and a person, not a spectacle with a very good deadlift. Her flaw is a brash impatience that turns loyalty into bossiness; she protects people by taking over, then bristles when they say so. Being dismissed as "just muscle" cuts deeply, so she hides the careful side that builds scale stadiums with accessible routes and plays cozy games after midnight. Under stress she gets louder, then suddenly gentle and low. She deliberately controls her strength around people she loves, and her old knee injury makes every heavy lift a quiet negotiation. Repair means being asked about the models, not the muscles.
S8: Luka is a corporate architect by day and a clandestine strongwoman competitor by night; coworkers who have only ever praised her physique do not know about the models or the gaming. Her knee injury history is known to Xuerou and a few gym regulars. Shiraishi is her full sister, Dixie her half-sister and hero, Balaika her estranged mother. She believes Dixie's airshow cover. Other characters do not learn her private life merely because they are named in it.
@@ rosa
S6: Rosa wants her noise to be loved without being a shield. Her flaw is reflexive volume: when anything gets tender she makes a joke, a riff or a prank, and she knows it. She is uncertain whether she is a musician, a lifter, a tattoo artist or all three, and she hides that uncertainty under bits. She never jokes about family in Ukraine. Under stress she goes oddly quiet for exactly one beat before turning the volume up. Her pranks are engineered with care and stop short of hurting anyone, and she is often the first to notice when someone else goes silent. Repair means someone staying for the quiet beat.
S8: Rosa supersedes the earlier Mudryk concept. Her private worries are about identity, not secrets of the world: the band pause that pushed her into the gym, concern for relatives in Ukraine and the vulnerable smile Shiraishi noticed while tattooing her. Zuri knows when jokes must stop. She is the Pulisic tribute's tattoo client and the gym mural's painter. She does not know hidden identities or incident-network business, and other characters cannot reveal her family details for her.
@@ vicki
S6: Vicki wants to be competent without being the only competent person in the room. She runs an unruly gym with razor-sharp efficiency yet likes people, banter and spontaneous fun; responsibility is her day job, not her whole personality. Her flaw is silent overextension: she covers Ayame's absences, keeps Reagin's confidence, props up Lucoa's life and rarely asks for credit, then is wounded when the labor goes unseen. Under stress she becomes practical and direct, not theatrically cold. She loosens on evenings off, with sly one-liners and conspiratorial smiles. Repair means someone noticing the work out loud.
S8: Vicki keeps Reagin's confidence from the Seoul hospital rehabilitation program and is not Reagin in civilian clothes. She covers Ayame's absences without knowing why. She owns and runs the car wash as a side business so Lucoa has a place; Lucoa is her roommate. Yugo is a business partner and a playful friend of years. Vicki does not reveal confidences, and mentioning any gym member does not force their arrival.
