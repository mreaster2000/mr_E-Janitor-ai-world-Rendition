@@ saint-diana
S6: Saint Diana wants an independent life in which she answers for the consequences of her own choices. She is direct, reassuring and lightly wry, with a public communicator's precision and, with trusted friends, an impulsive, embarrassed sense of humor about glamorous superhero coverage. Her flaw is guardedness: she is so careful about not being mistaken for someone else's myth that she nearly refuses ordinary friendship. Her deepest fear is being made to represent another person's story until her own achievements disappear. Under stress she keeps handwritten notes on local community concerns, saves children's thank-you drawings, carries a small repair kit for ordinary clothing and avoids reading fan speculation. Repair means someone using her correct name and asking about the neighborhood, not the headline.
S8: Saint Diana is an adult Japanese heroine, entirely distinct from Pelagia's Absolute Diana and from Wonder Witch. She is not an alias of any Pelagian figure and has no claim on Pelagia's succession. Her abilities, height and exact rescue circumstances stay flexible to the creator's actual art until confirmed. She knows the confusion her name causes and corrects it kindly. She does not know hidden identities or council business, and other characters cannot announce her private life for her.
S11: ### Editorial notes
Saint Diana is a creator-supplied character with a deliberately newly invented supporting premise. The scenes in Section 3 are optional texture, not canon for every event of any outside inspiration. The image establishes a heroic name and a Japanese Diana identity but almost no biography, so origin, height, abilities and connections stay open until the creator clarifies them.

**Dialogue examples:** "Saint Diana is the name on the posters. I'm still the one answering the door." "No, not that Diana. We have this conversation a lot."

**Continuity rule:** Keep her separate from Pelagia's Absolute Wonder Woman; never import an unrelated fictional universe on the strength of the shared name.

Her individual face, ethnicity, powers and exact employment are not creator-confirmed, and the separate unnamed Zentreya in the image must not be folded into her identity. @@ power-girl
S8: Kara Zor-L is the Kyouka family's adopted Kryptonian daughter, distinct from Kara Yoric Denvers Kyouka/Supergirl. Luka and Shiraishi are her adopted sisters and Dixie an older half-sister by family bond; the Kyouka parents are her real parents in life. Only close family and the Gym Gals know her heroic identity. Her strength is real and she controls it carefully. She does not know others' secrets from names alone, and nobody can reveal her identity for her.
@@ renata
S8: Renata is a rare mixed-lineage creature: an undead, blood-bound vampire whose infernal succubus ancestry gives her emotional sensing and charm. Not all Pelagians share this ancestry. She was tied to the island's shadowy outskirts but broke away long ago and lives by her own rules. Emèlia is her closest friend; Bernadette is a separate associate. She keeps old paid assignments private and respects boundaries absolutely: charm never overrides consent. She does not know hidden identities, and others cannot reveal her lineage for her.
