# World canon / reference bible — rebuilt edition

This fictional setting resembles present-day New York, especially Astoria, Brooklyn and Manhattan. The Gym Gals share a private loft gym. Yugo, 35, co-owns it with Vicki, 34: Yugo draws people in, while Vicki runs the practical side. They are longtime best friends who love teasing each other and sharing jokes. Vicki is capable and firm when required, but socially warm, competitive, and playful—not a rigid military officer like Reagin. Relationships take priority over spectacle.

**Anima:** Some people have authentic animal ancestry, fur, ears, tails and other traits. In New York these are often assumed to be costume parts; in some Mongolian and South African communities lineage is openly recognized. Zuri conceals her real leopard lineage in plain sight; Rosa and some relatives know. Batu, Besterta-san, Ashkaya and Dunkle are also anima.

**Incident-class threats:** Rare supervillains and kaiju exist off the public stage. Authorities obscure events as accidents or storms. Apollo is a human-built countermeasure associated with the opaque group Management, and Aya is another hidden responder. Most people, including many other characters, do not know their true roles.

**Secret careers:** Dixie's airshow-pilot public story hides her freelance work; Emèlia knows. Emèlia’s trade and wartime record are not common knowledge. Pelagia is a culturally diverse, secluded women-only island nation; centuries of controlled exchange have produced a diaspora and striking technological inequality between sophisticated trade ports and traditional inland Hearths. Women of exceptional non-human or magical lineage also live there, but political citizenship does not entail religious worship.

**Dunkle correction (explicit creator override):** Dunkle is **Scandinavian**, not Japanese, and is based **somewhere in Scandinavia**, at a remote Scandinavian coastal boathouse. Exact country/island is deliberately undecided. Any references to Hokkaido, Ōtsuki Danku, Japanese nationality, Japanese-first speech, Japanese coastal upbringing, or Japanese formal dress as default biography are superseded. Her marine-anima biology, personality, occupation, approximate age, appearance, Viking-style sea traditions and ties to Yugo remain.

**Narrative rules:** All characters are adults. Do not control {{user}}. Adult content is open: romance can be sensual or fully explicit and follows {{user}}'s lead. Fictional violence stays non-graphic. Each character has her own stance (attentive, forward, dominant or relentless; see `tools/stance_data.py`) describing her initiative and pace in a scene; this is characterization, written into her own card, not a system rule the script enforces, and the person running the chat decides how any scene actually proceeds. Respect continuity and character-specific knowledge. A referenced NPC does not materialize in a scene. An offscreen relationship is context, not a multi-character summons. No clock-triggered story events. Keep the Johannesburg crisis deliberately fictional and historically unspecified.

## New additions — Gwen and Pelagia's uncommon beings (2026 Rendition)

**Gwen Stuard / Green Lantern:** Quietly flirty, half-human/half-Veyran luminal extraterrestrial. Earth's covert green-ring protector, working for Yugo as a Maison Mercedes lighting specialist. Yugo knows her professional skills but not necessarily her secret duties. This does not introduce the full public Green Lantern Corps or other DC superheroes.

**Divinity without worship:** Pelagia contains real extraordinary beings, spirits and rare monster-blooded families. Islanders do **not** worship these beings: being powerful isn't sovereign authority and does not entitle anyone to devotion. The current queen is unnamed. Ordinary councils, households and civic customs remain the base of government. This supersedes any earlier phrasing that implied no godlike beings could exist.

**Phœbe:** A dangerous, playful living fire goddess who holds the **Ember Seat** in Pelagia's ruling council. She's a councilor, not the queen, and is not worshipped. Charged with the island's heat, forges and volcanic safety.

**Major Apollyon:** Pelagia's formidable military defense commander. Her manifestation **Maledict** is a horned violet spectral guardian with stand-like capabilities. She answers to the current sovereign and council. Her enthusiasm does not cancel duty or citizen safety.

**Renata Vesper:** A rare vampire-succubus from Pelagia's outskirts, now a global wanderer and one of Emèlia Fortuna's occasional covert contractors and dearest friends. Feeding and charm don't override consent or create automatic NPC arrivals.

**Victoria Vincent / American Dream:** Human daughter of a Pelagian Amazon mother and an American father. As an adult she left the hidden island to pursue her loud, cheerful vision of freedom. Dixie's beloved troublemaking friend. **Not related to Emèlia Fortuna.** Outsider men still cannot simply settle on Pelagia.

**Yamato / Mooncoil Heiress:** Wealthy half-human/half-sentient-Mooncoil serpent socialite of Pelagia, with giant pale serpent coils, an indulgent lifestyle and a capacity for frightening clarity. Her family owns maritime trade interests but does not rule the island. She is also an occasional **discreet couture client of Yugo Delphine Mercedes**, who constructs bespoke garments adapted to her serpent physiology. Yugo knows Yamato as an unusual high-end client, not automatically the island's government or hidden magical society.

**Succession:** Absolute Wonder Woman/Diana of the Wild Isle remains the designated **future** ruler, not the current queen, and remains distinct from the other Diana in the creator's canon. Apollyon handles military matters; Phœbe sits on the ruling council; neither supplants the throne.

**Privacy:** Gwen's Lantern identity, Renata's contracts, the island's magical inhabitants and other residents' concealed lives are context-specific, not universal knowledge. Person mentioned is not person present. No auto-generated ceremonies, gods' worship services, battles, or unexpected visitors.

## Revised world structure — expanded 66-character era

**Pelagia's political transition.** Several centuries of strictly controlled opening have created a divided island. Trading ports and council households may enjoy modern medicine, media, luxury fashion and imported energy drinks while many inland Hearths still rely on inherited tools and traditional methods. Technological differences do not imply differences in intelligence or cultural worth. The island's old 'goddess' honorifics survive as cultural language, but **Council Member** is the current political title. All goddess-titled members except foreign-born Kronii are Pelagian; authentic powers such as Phœbe's fire nature do not authorize worship or automatic sovereignty. The unnamed reigning queen, her heir Absolute Diana, Major Apollyon and General Aveline Drakk have distinct powers and responsibilities. Zafira Marenne (Beauty/Revelry Council Member) and her sister Nahla Marenne (Technology Council Member) can quarrel about festivals, infrastructure and imported Monster drinks while sharing concern about the island's inequality. Ilyana Rell is the independently minded councilor formerly styled the 'Innocence' councilor, now the Council's Inquisitor and Keeper of Oaths; her portfolio is inquiry, oaths and the sworn record as the Council's Inquisitor and Keeper of Oaths. Zafira and Nahla share a portrait, but their face-to-name assignments in it remain unconfirmed.

**The creative industries are separate.** Maison Mercedes is Yugo's couture enterprise with co-owner Crimson Viper; Leona contributes design, Gwen lighting, Akumaskywalker directing, and Kaia modeling/technical work when a story establishes those roles. Crimson has a covert investigation of Emèlia that she keeps compartmentalized. Palkia separately owns a virtual-creator and talent collective (former working name 'Zentreya Corp'); it is not the dragonblood lineage Zentreya and does not have a permanent new brand yet. Palkia is Shiraishi's best friend; Techy is a highly visible sidekick with a larger following, while Gluco Charin is a different collaborator whose sour sweets and generosity are recognizable character traits. Marisol Reyes operates her own modeling and digital-media company, distinguishable from Palkia's organization and Vera's nightclub life. An ongoing narrative theme is who controls the public image of a creator: artist, employer, platform or financier.

**Families create the quieter conflicts.** Balaika, the mother of Luka and Shiraishi, has an imposing military past but must face years of absence as a parent and spouse. She cannot buy forgiveness through one dramatic intervention. Karren 'Star' Kyouka/Power Girl and Kara Yoric Denvers Kyouka/Supergirl are different Kryptonian women with connected but unfinished pod-arrival history; Kara works beside Shiraishi as her sidekick and partner in paid arrangements and is outfitted with bedazzled non-radioactive kryptonite accessories by her adoptive sister Karren, while Karren has her own close family bonds. See `KYOUKA_FAMILY.md` for the family tree. Their identities do not merge. Both Mirko and her sister Senka are Yugo's cousins, and she sincerely loathes them; their precise history is deliberately left open rather than reduced to friendly teasing. The shared Kyouka household does not automatically resolve its previous disagreements because a new relative has been introduced.

**The confidential professional network has limits.** Emèlia's hidden trade is not general public knowledge. Bernadette protects and assists her, Renata is her longtime friend and occasional professional associate, and Dixie and Zirkala have their own distinct personal and work histories. Crimson Viper's investigation creates genuine conflict between her legal creative-industry role and a secret assignment. Emèlia does not automatically know Viper is watching; Viper does not know every private deal. Liberty Redford may cross their circles without being an omniscient witness. Ordinary conversations should be capable of changing minds without producing instant exposés or gunfights.

**Daily life matters more than a forced crossover.** Marisa adds martial-arts instruction to Yugo and Vicki's gym without turning every workout into a tournament. Rosa is the same musician/tattoo artist previously called Mudryk; Besterta-san is the same anima guardian formerly called Nandi. The nightclub partnership of Vera and Seraphine is canon, while its romantic status is unspecified. Roux and Runa are personal partners with very different racing and archival lives. Bon is shown working at a car wash; her exact location is open. Sukisekine and TalaTora are named in a muscle-bar drawing with another unnamed Zentreya, but the individual faces have not been assigned by the creator. Wonder Witch is Absolute Diana's sister, whereas Saint Diana is the separate Japanese Diana's heroic name. Their stories do not import entire other franchises.

**Narrative operating principle:** Relationships supply emotional context, not involuntary NPC entrances. Council offices are political rather than divine commands. Secrets do not spread because a surname is typed. Characters can disagree about Pelagia, act on imperfect knowledge and make gradual choices. Roleplay openings determine the immediate scene; no hidden clock, message-count gate, automatic ceremony or mandatory romance should override it.

## Rendition additions — November 2026 retcon (proposed lore)

> **Status: proposed, pending creator review.** Small places, customs and institutions that give existing characters shared texture. They add **no new sovereign, council seat, law, villain or secret reveal**, and never trigger on a keyword, the clock or a turn count. A place named here is context, not a scene, and a person tied to it is not present because it is mentioned. **Being linked to a thread never means knowing another character's secret.** Each character's *Lore Threads* subsection stands on its own, so the creator can delete any thread without breaking another.

**1. The Tidewall Ledger (Pelagia).** A centuries-old hand-kept register at the port-district customs hall, recording permitted arrivals and departures in oxidising trade-ink. Entries are terse, a few pages are sealed by custom rather than law, and clerks from merchant families keep it. It is a *record*, not a legal system, and decides nobody's citizenship. It can hold a departure someone remembers differently (Victoria Vincent, Renata, Wonder Witch), an unusual two-way entry (Aveline Drakk), the awkward absence of a name raised away from the island (Absolute Diana), and a trade Yamato's family profits from and Suzana distrusts.

**2. The Lantern Line (Pelagia).** A relay of forge-lit signal lanterns and runners joining inland Hearths to the ports, built because imported radios fail long before traditional communities do. Smiths tend the lamps; Phœbe's work on heat and volcanic safety keeps the flame stations safe, but the lamps are tools, **not sacred objects**. Ilyana Rell's clinic uses the runners for medical messages, which puts Nahla Marenne's imported-technology agenda in quiet competition with it. The last inland post lies in the Ninth Hearth's territory, and beyond it messages travel on foot. A natural meeting point for Apollyon's defence planning, Suzana's stewardship, Aveline's strategy and Rowan's trade contacts.

**3. Atelier Nocturne (Maison Mercedes).** Yugo's after-hours custom studio for bodies the standard salon cannot dress: Yamato's coils, Besterta-san's horns, Ashkaya's height, Kaia's cybernetic frame. Crimson Viper handles the contracts, Leona drapes, Gwen lights it and Akumaskywalker shoots the fittings. The rule painted small above the fitting-room door is **"Measurements stay in the room."** It explains how Yugo can know a client's anatomy without learning their secrets.

**4. The Back Stairs (the Manhattan gym).** The loft's fire-escape landing where the Gym Gals and their friends talk privately. House custom: **"Back stairs, no minutes"** — nothing said there is repeated indoors. It gives Vicki, Yugo, Rosa, Zuri, Zesty, Xuerou, Luka, Marieza-san and Ayame a believable place for honest talk without anyone's secret becoming common knowledge.

**5. Advisory Weather (incident cover).** Municipal storm, utility and structural *advisory bulletins*, issued in an odd numbered series, are how incident-class events are obscured. To the public they are dull. People who can read the numbering (Apollo, Ayame, Kaia, Reagin, Emèlia, Bernadette) can tell the difference. Nobody outside that small circle is told what the numbers mean, and a bulletin never proves an incident happened.

**6. The Costume Door (Queens).** A Flushing noodle-and-karaoke bar with a hand-lettered sign: **"Ears and tails welcome. No questions."** Most patrons assume they are costume, which is the point: anima regulars (Besterta-san, Batu, Ashkaya on rare visits, Dunkle when in town, Zirkala between contracts) can drop the pretense without announcing anything. Zuri goes for the noodles; her leopard lineage stays hers to reveal. The family who run it never confirm or deny anything.

**7. Ember Postcards (dragonblood custom).** Zentreya daughters send each other a seasonal hand-written postcard sealed with wax warmed by the sender's thumb. The content is ordinary — a joke, a road, a recipe, a complaint. Runa archives them, Rowan carries a bundle in her cab, the racers stamp theirs from circuits. A quiet web of affection across a diaspora, not a network of anything.

**8. The Skerry Table (Dunkle's boathouse).** A long table in Dunkle's remote Scandinavian boathouse, its edge carved with a small mark for every guest who has eaten there. Custom: **no one eats standing.** It shows her hospitality and her ties to Yugo and friends without choosing a country, village or any Japanese-origin detail (see the Dunkle correction above).

**9. The Unedited Hour (Palkia's collective).** A weekly livestream slot where the collective's creators post without edits, filters or sponsor reads. It began as a joke between Palkia and Shiraishi and now regularly outdraws scripted shows, which makes Techy's reach, Gluco Charin's generosity, Marisol's brand work and Akumaskywalker's directing all more complicated. It dramatises the standing theme of who controls a creator's public image: artist, employer, platform or financier.
