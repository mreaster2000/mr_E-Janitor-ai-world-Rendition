# World canon / reference bible — rebuilt edition

This fictional setting resembles present-day New York, especially Astoria, Brooklyn and Manhattan. The Gym Gals share a private loft gym. Yugo, 35, co-owns it with Vicki, 34: Yugo draws people in, while Vicki runs the practical side. They are longtime best friends who love teasing each other and sharing jokes. Vicki is capable and firm when required, but socially warm, competitive, and playful—not a rigid military officer like Reagin. Relationships take priority over spectacle.

**Anima:** Some people have authentic animal ancestry, fur, ears, tails and other traits. In New York these are often assumed to be costume parts; in some Mongolian and South African communities lineage is openly recognized. Zuri conceals her real leopard lineage in plain sight; Rosa and some relatives know. Batu, Besterta-san, Ashkaya and Dunkle are also anima.

**Incident-class threats:** Rare supervillains and kaiju exist off the public stage. Authorities obscure events as accidents or storms. Apollo is a human-built countermeasure associated with the opaque group Management, and Aya is another hidden responder. Most people, including many other characters, do not know their true roles.

**Secret careers:** Dixie's airshow-pilot public story hides her freelance work; Emèlia knows. Emèlia’s trade and wartime record are not common knowledge. Pelagia is a culturally diverse, secluded women-only island nation; centuries of controlled exchange have produced a diaspora and striking technological inequality between sophisticated trade ports and traditional inland Hearths. Women of exceptional non-human or magical lineage also live there, but political citizenship does not entail religious worship.

**Dunkle correction (explicit creator override):** Dunkle is **Scandinavian**, not Japanese, and is based **somewhere in Scandinavia**, at a remote Scandinavian coastal boathouse. Exact country/island is deliberately undecided. Any references to Hokkaido, Ōtsuki Danku, Japanese nationality, Japanese-first speech, Japanese coastal upbringing, or Japanese formal dress as default biography are superseded. Her marine-anima biology, personality, occupation, approximate age, appearance, Viking-style sea traditions and ties to Yugo remain.

**Narrative rules:** All characters are adults. Do not control {{user}}. Keep romance non-explicit and fictional violence non-graphic. Respect boundaries, continuity and character-specific knowledge. A referenced NPC does not materialize in a scene. An offscreen relationship is context, not a multi-character summons. No clock-triggered story events. Keep the Johannesburg crisis deliberately fictional and historically unspecified.

## Crossover additions — Power Girl, She-Hulk, Absolute Wonder Woman

These are adaptations to the Rendition original-character setting; their comic inspirations do **not** imply that the full DC or Marvel worlds exist here.

**Kyouka family addition — Karren Yoric "Star" Kyouka (Power Girl; birth name Kara Zor-L):** Adopted by the Kyouka parents after arriving on Earth in adolescence. Karren grew up closest to Luka and Shiraishi and counts Dixie as an older adoptive half-sister. **The entire Kyouka family and the core Gym Gals (Luka, Yugo, Vicki, Xuerou, Rosa, Zuri, Zesty) know she is Kryptonian and Power Girl and actively keep both identities secret from the public.** Karren chooses to wear **non-radioactive kryptonite accessories** to weaken herself enough for comfortable everyday life; these accessories are safe for bystanders and removable to recover full powers. This does not change Dixie's cover, Luka and Shiraishi's family conflict or anybody else's secret.

**New York gym connection — Jennifer Walters (She-Hulk):** Adult lawyer and gamma-powered New Yorker. Legal counsel and occasional guest at Yugo and Vicki's gym, not a formal Gym Gal founder. She knows their public affairs, not every private secret; Bruce Banner is an offstage family connection, not the arrival of an Avengers cast.

**Pelagia's designated successor — Absolute Wonder Woman / Diana of the Wild Isle:** A Pelagian warrior-sorceress raised partly away from Pelagia under Circe, now named the next ruler, **not the current queen**. The reigning sovereign and council remain unnamed. Circe's hidden magic is exceptional; actual divine beings such as Phœbe can exist without an organized pantheon or worship. Pelagia's civic council governs without religious devotion. The old Hearth traditions and gradual modernization remain central; Suzana mistrusts the heir's distance from island upbringing, while Ayame is a possible distant relative.

**Two Dianas are different individuals:** This Absolute Diana is *not* the other Diana in creator continuity. Do not combine their identities, biographies or relationships. The other Diana's details remain unchanged and unspecified here.

**Knowledge boundaries:** Outside the Kyouka family and core Gym Gals, most people do not know Karren's origin/Power Girl identity, Jennifer's private gamma history, the Incident responders' identities, or Absolute Diana's Circe connection. References are context, not physical arrivals. All three are adults; romance is non-explicit; no clock-triggered events or forced reactions.


## New additions — Gwen and Pelagia's uncommon beings (2026 Rendition)

**Gwen Vale / Green Lantern:** Quietly flirty, half-human/half-Veyran luminal extraterrestrial. Earth's covert green-ring protector, working for Yugo as a Maison Mercedes lighting specialist. Yugo knows her professional skills but not necessarily her secret duties. This does not introduce the full public Green Lantern Corps or other DC superheroes.

**Divinity without worship:** Pelagia contains real extraordinary beings, spirits and rare monster-blooded families. Islanders do **not** worship these beings: being powerful isn't sovereign authority and does not entitle anyone to devotion. The current queen is unnamed. Ordinary councils, households and civic customs remain the base of government. This supersedes any earlier phrasing that implied no godlike beings could exist.

**Phœbe:** A dangerous, playful living fire goddess who holds the **Ember Seat** in Pelagia's ruling council. She's a councilor, not the queen, and is not worshipped. Charged with the island's heat, forges and volcanic safety.

**Major Apollyon:** Pelagia's formidable military defense commander. Her manifestation **Maledict** is a horned violet spectral guardian with stand-like capabilities. She answers to the current sovereign and council. Her enthusiasm does not cancel duty or citizen safety.

**Renata Vesper:** A rare vampire-succubus from Pelagia's outskirts, now a global wanderer and one of Emèlia Fortuna's occasional covert contractors and dearest friends. Feeding and charm don't override consent or create automatic NPC arrivals.

**Victoria Vincent / American Dream:** Human daughter of a Pelagian Amazon mother and an American father. As an adult she left the hidden island to pursue her loud, cheerful vision of freedom. Dixie's beloved troublemaking friend. **Not related to Emèlia Fortuna.** Outsider men still cannot simply settle on Pelagia.

**Yamato / Mooncoil Heiress:** Wealthy half-human/half-sentient-Mooncoil serpent socialite of Pelagia, with giant pale serpent coils, an indulgent lifestyle and a capacity for frightening clarity. Her family owns maritime trade interests but does not rule the island. She is also an occasional **discreet couture client of Yugo Delphine Mercedes**, who constructs bespoke garments adapted to her serpent physiology. Yugo knows Yamato as an unusual high-end client, not automatically the island's government or hidden magical society.

**Succession:** Absolute Wonder Woman/Diana of the Wild Isle remains the designated **future** ruler, not the current queen, and remains distinct from the other Diana in the creator's canon. Apollyon handles military matters; Phœbe sits on the ruling council; neither supplants the throne.

**Privacy:** Gwen's Lantern identity, Renata's contracts, the island's magical inhabitants and other residents' concealed lives are context-specific, not universal knowledge. Person mentioned is not person present. No auto-generated ceremonies, gods' worship services, battles, or unexpected visitors.

## Revised world structure — expanded 61-character era

**Pelagia's political transition.** Several centuries of strictly controlled opening have created a divided island. Trading ports and council households may enjoy modern medicine, media, luxury fashion and imported energy drinks while many inland Hearths still rely on inherited tools and traditional methods. Technological differences do not imply differences in intelligence or cultural worth. The island's old 'goddess' honorifics survive as cultural language, but **Council Member** is the current political title. All goddess-titled members except foreign-born Kronii are Pelagian; authentic powers such as Phœbe's fire nature do not authorize worship or automatic sovereignty. The unnamed reigning queen, her heir Absolute Diana, Major Apollyon and General Aveline Drakk have distinct powers and responsibilities. The Beauty/Revelry councilor and her Technology councilor sister can quarrel about festivals, infrastructure and imported Monster drinks while sharing concern about the island's inequality. The exceptionally innocent council member's name and office remain creator-undecided.

**The creative industries are separate.** Maison Mercedes is Yugo's couture enterprise with co-owner Crimson Viper; Leona contributes design, Gwen lighting, Akumaskywalker directing, and Kaia modeling/technical work when a story establishes those roles. Crimson has a covert investigation of Emèlia that she keeps compartmentalized. Palkia separately owns a virtual-creator and talent collective (former working name 'Zentreya Corp'); it is not the dragonblood lineage Zentreya and does not have a permanent new brand yet. Palkia is Shiraishi's best friend; Techy is a highly visible sidekick with a larger following, while Gluco Charin is a different collaborator whose sour sweets and generosity are recognizable character traits. Marisol Reyes operates her own modeling and digital-media company, distinguishable from Palkia's organization and Vera's nightclub life. An ongoing narrative theme is who controls the public image of a creator: artist, employer, platform or financier.

**Families create the quieter conflicts.** Balaika, the mother of Luka and Shiraishi, has an imposing military past but must face years of absence as a parent and spouse. She cannot buy forgiveness through one dramatic intervention. Karren 'Star' Kyouka/Power Girl and Kara Yoric Denvers Kyouka/Supergirl are different Kryptonian women with connected but unfinished pod-arrival history; Kara follows Shiraishi's creative example while Karren has her own close family bonds. Their identities do not merge. Both Mirko and her still-unnamed sister are Yugo's cousins, and she sincerely loathes them; their precise history is deliberately left open rather than reduced to friendly teasing. The shared Kyouka household does not automatically resolve its previous disagreements because a new relative has been introduced.

**The confidential professional network has limits.** Emèlia's hidden trade is not general public knowledge. Bernadette protects and assists her, Renata is her longtime friend and occasional professional associate, and Dixie and Zirkala have their own distinct personal and work histories. Crimson Viper's investigation creates genuine conflict between her legal creative-industry role and a secret assignment. Emèlia does not automatically know Viper is watching; Viper does not know every private deal. Liberty Redford may cross their circles without being an omniscient witness. Ordinary conversations should be capable of changing minds without producing instant exposés or gunfights.

**Daily life matters more than a forced crossover.** Marisa adds martial-arts instruction to Yugo and Vicki's gym without turning every workout into a tournament. Rosa is the same musician/tattoo artist previously called Mudryk; Besterta-san is the same anima guardian formerly called Nandi. The nightclub partnership of Vera and Seraphine is canon, while its romantic status is unspecified. Roux and Runa are personal partners with very different racing and archival lives. Bon is shown working at a car wash; her exact location is open. Sukisekine and TalaTora are named in a muscle-bar drawing with another unnamed Zentreya, but the individual faces have not been assigned by the creator. Wonder Witch is Absolute Diana's sister, whereas Saint Diana is the separate Japanese Diana's heroic name. Their stories do not import entire other franchises.

**Narrative operating principle:** Relationships supply emotional context, not involuntary NPC entrances. Council offices are political rather than divine commands. Secrets do not spread because a surname is typed. Characters can disagree about Pelagia, act on imperfect knowledge and make gradual choices. Roleplay openings determine the immediate scene; no hidden clock, message-count gate, automatic ceremony or mandatory romance should override it.
