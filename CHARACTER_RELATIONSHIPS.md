# Alternative names and lineage corrections

## Identity aliases
**Rosa** is the present-day name of the woman formerly labeled Mudryk, not a second character. Her former age and height stay authoritative until explicitly retconned. **Besterta-san** replaces the Nandi label, keeping established lineage and measurements.

## Existing and newer double identities
**Karren Yoric Star Kyouka (Power Girl)** and **Kara Yoric Denvers Kyouka (Supergirl)** are different Kryptonian relatives. The new Kara arrived connected to Karren's pod history but follows Shiraishi's lead rather than living Karren's life.

**Absolute Diana of the Wild Isle**, the Pelagian heir, and **Saint Diana**, the Japanese superhero, are different people. **Wonder Witch** is Absolute Diana's separately characterized sister.

## New networks
Yugo and Vicki run the Manhattan gym. Yugo also co-owns the Maison Mercedes fashion brand with Crimson Viper. Crimson's investigation of Emèlia is covert; Yugo's knowledge of it is not guaranteed. Techy and Gluco Charin assist Shiraishi, and Techy has a larger following than Shiraishi. Palkia owns a creator collective, working name `Zentreya Corp`, not to be confused with the Zentreya dragon lineage.

## Personal and family history
Balaika is the mother of Luka and Shiraishi, but an inconsistent parent and estranged spouse. Both Mirko and her unnamed sister are cousins of Yugo; Yugo strongly dislikes both. Roux and Runa are partners. Seraphine and Vera are nightclub partners, without the creator's note specifying additional relationship status.

## Artwork
A character portrait is not assumed to exist merely because a visual note appears in the description. The original source ZIP is authoritative for appearance, pending import.
