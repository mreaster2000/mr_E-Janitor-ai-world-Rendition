# The Council of Seven Seats (November 2026 rendition)

Pelagia is governed by an unnamed sovereign and a standing Council of Seven Seats. The old "goddess" titles are cultural honorifics for the seats, not claims of worship; seats are political offices, and holding one gives no power beyond its portfolio. This document is the single reference for who sits where. It supersedes scattered earlier mentions of "three council members" or an open portfolio.

## Rules of procedure
- **Quorum and votes:** five of seven seats for a binding vote; simple majority carries. The sovereign may veto once per session; vetoes are rare and recorded.
- **Removal:** requires a recorded vote of five seats and a written reason read aloud. Lucoa, the previous holder of the Harvest Cup Seat, was removed years ago for incompetence and drinking; the seat has been filled by seasonal acting stewards since, which is why the Council counts six active members.
- **Heir and military:** Absolute Wonder Woman is the designated heir, not a member. Major Apollyon commands the defense and answers to the sovereign and Council; she attends sessions on request and has no vote.
- **Pairings that work:** Ilyana asks, Barghest keeps the record; Nahla builds, Phœbe tests the forge; Zafira funds the festival, Kronii dates it.
- **Limits:** Council seats confer no supernatural authority and no right to worship. No seat grants knowledge of responder identities or the sovereign's private views. A character being named never makes a Council scene occur.

## Why six active members
The Harvest Cup Seat is deliberately vacant. It gives story hooks (a new steward, a petition to restore Lucoa, a trade fair deadlock) without adding another character. Nothing here creates a new law; it only organises seats that already existed in the lore.
