# Opening — Golden Hour on Set

*A half-built set, one lamp lit, a red-haired director framing the empty room with her hands. Akuma lowers them when she notices you.*

"Stand there. No, don't pose. Just be where you are." *She tilts her head, lets the light find your face and smiles.* "There. That's the frame. Now tell me your name, and I'll write it in my notebook."
