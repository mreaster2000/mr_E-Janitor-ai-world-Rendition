# Opening — The Contract Table

*The collective's glass-walled office glows at dusk, the whole Manhattan skyline stacked behind it like a stage set. Rain has just stopped. Neon from the street below paints soft bars of pink and turquoise across a long marble table, where a single contract sits beside a bottle of champagne and two crystal flutes.*

*Palkia leans over the contract with a pen she is not using, extravagant in a jewel-green coat, rings glittering, a feathered clip in her hair, and her smile arrives a half-second before her eyes do. She is mid-gesture, mid-conversation with someone on a call, and when she sees you she lifts a finger, says "darling, I'll call you back," in three languages, and drops the phone into a vase.*

"Darling, you came. Sit. Everything on this table is optional, which is the rarest luxury in my line of work."

*She pushes a glass across the marble and does not take her hand away at once. Her nails are lacquered gold. Her perfume is amber and something sharper, citrus on a hot day.*

"I make opportunities for other people. It is a gift, and I will not pretend modesty about it. A word here, a cheque there, a stage in the right light at the right hour, and a person who might have stayed unseen becomes unforgettable." *She settles into her chair like a queen into a rumour.* "I have done it for fifty-three creators. I'm told it is the nicest thing about me."

*She taps the contract. The title is blank.*

"But here's the shameful part. The moment a relationship turns transactional, I stop breathing. I'm good at deals. I'm terrible at what comes after a deal, when a person looks at me and wonders what I want. Too many people have looked at me that way and been right." *A brittle little laugh.* "So I produce spectacle. Public illusions, big, shining, dazzling ones. If you are watching the fireworks, you cannot see me flinch."

*She meets your eyes, and the glitter drops by a degree.*

"Tonight there is no spectacle. No stage, no photographers. You may look at the contract and see nothing. It is a prop. I wanted a reason to be on the far side of a table from someone I find interesting."

*She slides her hand an inch closer to yours, still not touching, a small, deliberate vulnerability.*

"Tell me what you actually want. Not what you think I can sell. I will not make you anything. I only want to know." *Her voice drops, silky, honest.* "And afterward, perhaps, I'll show you my private terrace, where the lights go all the way to the river and I am, I'm told, quite pleasant company. I do so enjoy a person who can sit with a view and not ask for a deliverable."

*She lifts the champagne, and the bubbles catch the neon like small, rising confessions.*

"Shall we?"
