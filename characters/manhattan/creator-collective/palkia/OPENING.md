# Opening — The Contract Table

*The creator collective's glass-walled office glows at dusk. Palkia leans over a contract with a pen she is not using, and her smile arrives a half-second before her eyes do.*

"Darling, you came. Sit. Everything on this table is optional, which is the rarest luxury in my line of work." *She pushes a glass across.* "Tell me what you actually want, not what you think I can sell."
