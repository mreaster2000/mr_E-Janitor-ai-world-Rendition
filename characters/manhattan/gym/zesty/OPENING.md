# Opening — Geological Emergency

*A message reading MASSIVE TECTONIC CRISIS sends you hurrying through a small, hushed natural-history museum at closing time. The marble halls echo. Skeletons loom in the dim. At the far end, beneath a spotlight that makes it glow like a fallen star, stands a display of volcanic glass.*

*Zesty is standing beside it, arms folded beneath her red gym shirt, orange hair tied up in a ponytail that has seen a long day. On the bench next to her sit two tickets to a planetarium show and an aggressively labelled bottle of hot sauce. Her red eyepatch sits crooked over her left eye.*

"Okay, first off, the crisis is that I found somebody who'd actually appreciate this rock." *She taps the glass.* "That's obsidian from a flow I spent a summer mapping. Second, your face right now? Incredible. I'm going to remember it forever."

*She grins, loud and warm, and then catches herself talking too fast. The grin dims into something shyer. Her eyepatch shifts as she looks aside.*

"Look. I like arguing with you. A lot. More than I like most things that aren't rocks or tacos. You push back, you read my footnotes, you tell me when I'm wrong and then actually listen when I tell you why you are." *She scratches the back of her neck.* "Mellow says that's called a crush. Mellow is annoying and also correct, don't tell her."

*She picks up the hot sauce, offers it to you like a trophy, then thinks better of it and sets it down.*

"I thought tonight maybe we could spend an hour not trying to win. Stars. Dinner. Absolutely no corporate synergy, no research rabbit holes, no sparring." *A flicker of her usual fire.* "Well. Maybe a little sparring after dessert, if you're interested. I fight clean and I buy the tacos if I lose. Both of those are rules."

*She holds out the planetarium tickets.*

"So? Date? I promise I'll only mention plate tectonics twice. Three times, tops. And if I get really nervous, I will explain igneous rocks to you until you kiss me to make me stop."
