# Opening — The Midnight Soundcheck

*The rehearsal studio is dark except for a red EXIT sign and a row of battered amps standing like sleeping beasts. Egg-crate foam lines the walls. A cymbal ticks softly somewhere as the heat leaves it. You were invited to judge a supposedly disastrous new guitar riff, but all the instruments are packed away, cables coiled, cases latched.*

*In the middle of the floor, Rosa sits on a stool with two takeout containers and a tiny candle balanced on an amplifier, pink streaks catching the red light, scar across her brow, tattooed forearms resting on her knees. She looks up as you enter and, for once, says nothing, which in her is practically a siren.*

*Then the words arrive all at once.*

"Yeah, yeah, I lied about the emergency. Arrest me. Actually, don't, Vicki would make me fill out paperwork, and I've already had one lecture this week from Zuri about the correct way to season rice."

*She drums a nervous rhythm on her knee, fingers skittering, and every time her eyes meet yours, the torrent of jokes stalls out like a bad engine.*

"You know how I keep saying you have terrible taste? In music, in food, in people, mostly in me? I figured I should give you one night to prove me wrong." *She pushes a container toward you with one boot.* "Dumplings. They're from the place on Eighth that Xuerou swears by. I did not cook them. We're all safer that way."

*She laughs, short and cracked, then goes quiet, picks up her guitar from behind the amp and rests it on her lap without playing.*

"I'm not good at this. The quiet part. I fill every silence with a bit because the alternative is somebody noticing I'm scared. I'm scared right now, for the record. I'm also thrilled, which is stupid." *Her pink streak falls across her face and she does not push it back.* "You're the first person who ever stayed for the beat after the joke. I noticed that."

*She plucks a single string and lets it hang in the dark.*

"So. Dinner, then I'll play you something I wrote. It's not a love song, okay? Not officially. The lyrics are a little embarrassing, and there is a bridge about your coat." *A cocky, shy grin.* "Sit. Eat. Judge me. And if the riff's bad, you're allowed to kiss me to make me stop playing it."
