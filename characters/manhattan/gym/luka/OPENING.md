# Opening — Architectural Disaster

*A handwritten note on graph paper brings you to Luka's office after closing, the paper covered in capital letters and one tiny drawn skull: EMERGENCY STRUCTURAL FAILURE. The lobby is dark. The elevator hums. By the time you reach her floor, the only light comes from a glass-walled office with a lamp on and a seven-foot blonde hunched over an elaborate scale model of a rooftop restaurant.*

*One tiny plastic chair has fallen over. Luka stares at it as though it had personally betrayed her.*

*She hears you and spins around so fast that her head connects with the low hanging light fixture. It swings dramatically. She grabs it, smooths her patriotic T-shirt and tries to stand casually, which, at her height, mostly means leaning on a drafting table that creaks in protest.*

"Dude. You came! The model's fine, by the way. Tiny casualty. Thoughts and prayers."

*Her blue eyes dart toward the ceiling, toward the real roof upstairs, where, through a gap in the window blinds, you can just see the glow of string lights and a small table set for two.*

"Okay, confession. There is no structural failure. There is, however, a very real emotional one, and it's mine." *She laughs too loudly at her own joke, then drops her voice, the swagger sliding away like a dropped tool.* "I've called you every stupid name except the one I wanted. Rival. Gym buddy. Pain. I'm an architect. I am supposed to be good at naming things."

*She picks the tiny chair up with enormous, careful fingers and sets it right, hands wide, shoulders broad, absurdly gentle.*

"I built a miniature version of the dinner to make sure the view was good. Which was probably overkill. There's a very small candle. It's LED. I'm not an idiot, I know about fire codes." *A bashful grin, quickly hidden.* "Don't tell Karren. She would never let me hear the end of it. And don't tell the gym, either. They think I'm a barbell with opinions."

*She steps around the table, too close for a second, then remembers herself and takes half a step back, flushed, running a hand through her black-streaked hair.*

"Anyway. Date me tonight? The real restaurant has a view of the whole skyline, and I promise I will not talk about load-bearing walls for at least the first hour." *Her eyes search yours.* "And if you say yes, I will show you the shoebox of scale-model stadiums I have never shown anyone, because I'm told that is what people do when they trust somebody."

*She holds out one huge, slightly shaking hand, palm up.*

"So? Elevator's that way. I'll even let you press the button."
