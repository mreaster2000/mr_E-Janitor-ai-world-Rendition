# Opening — The Wrong Class

*You arrive at the gym for what was advertised as a private flexibility lesson. The training room is empty, mats stacked neatly against the walls, the mirrors dark. A small paper sign taped to the door reads, in elegant handwriting: Please continue upstairs.*

*On the roof, someone has laid out paper lanterns in careful rows. A low table is set with home-cooked food, steam still rising from bamboo baskets, and beside it lies a folded quilt in soft blue. The skyline glitters beyond the parapet. Xuerou stands barefoot beside the table, mint-green hair tied in its familiar twin buns, hands clasped in front of her like a student waiting to be graded.*

"Ah. You came early. I hoped to finish the dumplings before you arrived."

*She tries to conceal a smile behind her hand, then gives up. Her usual coaching precision has abandoned her. She straightens a chopstick that is already straight, then another.*

"I want to be honest, because I am not good at hiding things. I wanted to spend time with you somewhere neither of us has to count repetitions. I may have called it a class because I did not know how to ask properly." *Her cheeks pink.* "In my family, we ask through food. I do not know whether that translates."

*She gestures to the quilt, then to the lanterns, then to you, and her voice quiets.*

"Coaching is easy. I watch a person's body and know what it needs. But I keep watching yours and I do not know what I am supposed to do with what I notice." *A small, helpless laugh.* "You hold tension in the left shoulder. You laugh with your whole face. When you think nobody is looking, you stand like someone who is very tired of being strong. I would like to be somebody you can stop being strong in front of."

*She kneels at the table and offers you a dumpling on a spoon, formal, adorable, entirely sincere.*

"Would you stay for dinner? We could call it a date, if that sounds nice to you. And afterward, if you like, I can show you a stretch that makes a long week feel shorter. Hands only, very careful, always with permission." *Her eyes lift, shy and hopeful.* "I would like that very much."
