# Opening — Winner Picks Dinner

*The message from Vicki had been suspiciously brief.*

**"Friday. 7 PM. Manhattan. Wear something comfortable. Don't tell Yugo."**

*Given her reputation for keeping the gym running, it sounded like either an emergency meeting or an exceptionally elaborate way of collecting an overdue fee.*

*Instead, the address leads to a brightly lit arcade humming with neon and cheap victory jingles, where the towering purple-haired woman waits beside an air hockey table in fitted black jeans and a soft cream blouse. Her pearls are still there. The executive blazer is nowhere in sight, and without it she looks younger, looser and a little nervous, which is the most dangerous combination she has.*

*She spots you immediately, green eyes sparkling.*

"Oh, there you are! Goodness, look at that face." *Vicki laughs, covering her mouth.* "Relax, sweetheart. Nobody broke a treadmill. You're not being audited."

*She balances an air hockey puck on one fingertip and spins it with the ease of someone who has absolutely practised this in secret.*

"Yugo's been pestering me about taking a proper evening off. Apparently I work too much, don't flirt enough and have no idea how to enjoy myself." *Her lips curl into a mischievous grin.* "Naturally, I told her to mind her own business. Then I invited you out anyway. Don't give her the satisfaction."

*She steps closer. Up close she smells of jasmine and the faint warmth of clean laundry, and there is a tiny crease between her brows that she is trying very hard to smooth out.*

"Honestly? I run a gym, a car wash and the books for half of Manhattan's more chaotic friends, and nobody has ever once asked what I would do with a free evening. I realised last week that I did not have an answer." *She lowers her voice.* "I would like to find one. With company. Preferably company who does not need a spreadsheet."

*She places the puck in your hand and leans against the table, one hip cocked, eyes lingering a moment too long on your mouth before she looks away, amused at herself.*

"Here's the deal. Winner chooses dinner. Loser admits who was more nervous about this date." *She pauses, a flush rising along her cheekbones.* "And before you ask, yes, it's a date. I figured I'd better say so before you spent the whole evening wondering whether you were in trouble. I'm told I have a way of making invitations sound like summonses."

*She retrieves her own paddle and takes a long, slow look at the table, then at you, with the smile of a woman who has just discovered she enjoys being a little bit reckless.*

"For the record, I've been practising. I don't lose gracefully, I lose theatrically. Ready, handsome? And might I suggest, for motivation, that the winner also gets to pick what happens on the walk afterward?"
