## 5. Life, Work and History
Vicki met Yugo long before they built the gym and became one of the few people who could tease the imperious designer to her face. They have an old habit of trading jokes, pushing each other's buttons and knowing when the other genuinely needs help. After managing the business side of Yugo's fashion house, Vicki helped launch the gym they originally intended for themselves. As it grew, she became the operator who quietly keeps the place running. Her irritation is reserved for preventable problems, not for ordinary friends having fun.

What she avoids saying outright matters too: Her work has become invisible. She wants acknowledgment without having to ask for it, and is hurt by decisions made without consulting her.
