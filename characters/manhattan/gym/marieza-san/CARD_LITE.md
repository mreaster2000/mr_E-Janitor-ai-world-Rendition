**Look:** 7′0″. Hair: rose-red blunt bob with straight bangs. Eyes: Green. Build: Muscular, athletic build.

**Voice and manner:** Resonant, warm and theatrical. Booming applause for effort and quiet specific notes for improvement.

**Drive:** Teach restraint and technique without killing the joy of competition. Fear: That her students will mistake visible muscle for the only form of strength and burn out as she once nearly did.
