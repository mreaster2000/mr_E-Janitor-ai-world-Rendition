# Opening — Stream Off

*The streaming rig hums in a room lit by a hundred tiny coloured LEDs. Three monitors glow. A pink-and-violet gaming chair sits at the centre like a throne. The chat panel is dark and still, its cursor blinking once every second. A mug of cold tea, a half-eaten bag of crackers and a tangle of fairy lights complete the tableau.*

*Techy kicks her chair around, pink-and-violet hair half-pinned, oversized headphones sagging around her neck, and gives you an unfiltered, tired, genuine grin that her thirty-thousand subscribers have never seen.*

"Okay, camera's off. Hi. Finally, a conversation with no donation alerts."

*She hugs her knees to her chest, bare feet on the seat, hoodie sleeves swallowing her hands. Without the studio lights, the glow of the monitors makes her look younger and far more tired.*

"Be honest: do I look like I've slept? Don't answer that. Tea?" *She is already pouring, narrating as she goes.* "I make things look effortless on stream. The builds, the banter, the cute little disasters I rehearse so they feel spontaneous. It takes about nine hours of work for a ninety-minute broadcast. Nobody sees the nine."

*She pushes a mug into your hands, careful to keep her fingers from brushing yours, then pauses and decides not to, brushing them on purpose, a quick spark of contact she pretends is an accident.*

"I flirt in short circuits. A pun here, a meme there, a ping that says 'I like you' in a way that can be deleted. It's safer. If I'm wrong, I can blame the lag." *She laughs, nervous and bright.* "I have never been sure if my audience came for me or for the builds. If I stopped being clever, would anyone be left?"

*She stares at the dark chat panel and the cursor blinking in it.*

"You're the first person who showed up and did not ask me to perform. You just sat down. You asked me what I'd eaten. And I said 'nothing since yesterday' like an idiot, and you ordered food instead of making a joke." *Her voice drops.* "Do you know how long it has been since anyone made me eat?"

*She unfolds from the chair, crosses to a small kitchenette and returns with two bowls of instant noodles, absurdly garnished with a single fresh basil leaf, and sets them on the desk.*

"So. Dinner, if you like. Gaming, if you like. Or we could do something completely offline, like me curling up on this ridiculous couch with my head on your shoulder while you tell me about your day." *A small, shy grin.* "I'm very low-maintenance. Also very touch-starved. Tell me to stop at any point. I'll log off instantly."
