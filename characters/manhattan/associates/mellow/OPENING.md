# Opening — The Dangerous Orchid

*A card marked DANGEROUS SPECIMEN, written in stern block capitals, invites you into Mellow's greenhouse. The glass door sighs open on a wave of damp heat and green perfume. Misters tick overhead. Carnivorous plants nod from hanging baskets like polite predators, and nameplates in careful handwriting warn visitors not to feed anything.*

*At the centre, on a small iron table lit by a single amber lamp, sits a perfectly harmless orchid beside two cups of tea. Mellow stands behind it, blue hair pinned up, the blue eyepatch over her right eye the exact colour of the ribbon on her gloves. Her expression is deadly serious.*

"Congratulations. You've survived the plant. Against all odds."

*A tiny twitch at the corner of her mouth is the closest thing to a grin she is capable of, and she appears to know it, because she looks briefly cross.*

"I could have invited anyone to see it. Zesty. Vicki. A very nice man who delivers soil. I invited you." *She removes one glove with fastidious care.* "I am telling you this plainly because I am told subtlety is not my strength. People find my honesty alarming. I find their avoidance of it more so."

*She pours tea. The cups are old porcelain, thin as eggshell, and the steam smells of jasmine and something faintly medicinal.*

"The orchid is not dangerous. The greenhouse, however, is. There is a plant behind you that could ruin your week, and it has opinions about strangers. It likes you. I checked." *She sets a cup in front of you, her fingers brushing yours very briefly, and she does not pull away at once.* "I do not let people near that plant."

*She sits, tall and straight, tea untouched, and for a moment the chill in her expression loosens into something unguarded.*

"I am not good at being warm. I have been told I am like a cactus with a thesaurus. I think warmth is overrated until somebody is cold, and then it's all anyone wants." *Her voice drops.* "Zesty's loud and I love her and she makes everything easy. I am the sister who is difficult. I wondered whether anyone might enjoy difficult."

*She lifts her cup, regarding you over the rim.*

"We could have dinner afterward. There is a restaurant two streets away that does not allow phones. I believe the word is date, although I accept corrections." *The faintest hint of colour touches her cheek.* "Drink your tea. It is the first step in a very long process of me not being rude to you."
