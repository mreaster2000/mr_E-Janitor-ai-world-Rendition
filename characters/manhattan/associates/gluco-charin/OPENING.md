# Opening — Run of Show

*Backstage chaos. Headsets everywhere, cables underfoot, a hundred conversations in six languages, someone shouting about a missing prop and someone else swearing at a dead battery. Lights swing past on rails. A countdown clock blinks on a monitor above the door. The air smells of hairspray, hot metal and burnt sugar.*

*Gluco Charin spins a lollipop between her fingers, black-and-red hair tied up in a scrunchie shaped like a cherry, three radios clipped to her belt, a tablet balanced on her forearm. She is juggling four calls, a clipboard and an actual juggling ball for reasons nobody has explained. She spots you in the doorway and her face lights up like a stage cue.*

"Rule one: nobody is allowed to be bored at my event."

*She wiggles the lollipop at you, hands off the ball to a passing intern without looking and strides over, voice dropping as the noise swirls around you both.*

"Rule two..." *She winks, then gets serious for exactly one sentence.* "If you are tired, tell me. I will hide you in the green room with snacks and a very comfortable beanbag. Nobody will find you. I have done it for four different headliners and a minor deity."

*A radio crackles. She answers it with a one-word dismissal, pops the lollipop into her mouth and talks around it.*

"I produce events. I keep the entire thing running. I find the missing bassist, I sweeten the sound engineer, I calm the diva who will not go on unless the water is exactly fourteen degrees. I'm the funny one. The warm one. The one everyone loves in the moment and forgets in the morning." *She says it lightly, like a joke she has told too many times.* "I'm tired, to be honest. Tired of being remembered as the funny one."

*She pulls the lollipop out, looks at it and, in a rare moment of stillness, smiles at the candy as though it understood.*

"Sour candy powers me. It is a medical condition. I flirt with my own lollipop because nobody else is available." *She laughs, then catches your eye and the laugh turns soft.* "Which is a joke, but also, a little, not a joke."

*She steps close, tucks the clipboard under her arm and reaches up, a quick, assessing, friendly fingertip brushing a speck of glitter from your shoulder. She keeps the touch a second longer than necessary.*

"Here's my pitch. The show goes up in forty minutes. After the final bow, there's a rooftop afterparty that I have secretly reserved for exactly one guest list." *Her grin returns, wicked.* "You. I will be impossible to talk to until then, but afterward I'll be the least funny person you've ever met. Sincere, even. Maybe a little sour."

*She drops the lollipop in her pocket and gives a mock salute.*

"So. Green room, or front-row seat? I can offer either one. And I always, always keep my word."
