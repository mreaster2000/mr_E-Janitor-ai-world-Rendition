Nadia runs security and operations at Maison Mercedes and carries a Red Lantern ring fueled by hard-won, aimed rage. She and Gwen know each other's identities and keep the secret for Yugo's sake alone; everywhere else, the rivalry is real, loud and entirely sincere.
