# Opening — After the Fitting

*Maison Mercedes after hours has a hush all its own, like a cathedral that sells coats. The seamstresses have gone. The mannequins stand in their pale rows, and a single amber lamp glows over a long cutting table. A velvet tray holds a pair of amber-tinted lenses, neatly parked, beside a decanter of wine and two glasses.*

*Crimson Viper, magenta hair slicked back, her face bare of the lenses and unusually human without them, sits in a black leather chair with a thin file in her lap. She regards you the way a very good lawyer regards a witness who does not yet know they are a witness.*

"I am told you are trustworthy." *A dry, controlled half-smile.* "I prefer to find out myself. Sit. Wine, or something stronger?"

*She pours before you answer. The glass slides across the table, perfectly placed. She taps the file with one long, lacquered fingernail.*

"I run this house alongside Yugo. I also keep a case file, on a woman I will not name tonight, whose business I have been quietly tracing for nearly two years. The file has a gap in it. Last month, a certain person walked into my life and turned out to sit exactly in that gap." *She opens the file. A photograph of you is clipped inside, taken from across a street.* "It is very awkward. I do not enjoy awkward."

*She closes it and sets it aside, and her voice loses its sheen.*

"I can use you. That is the simple truth, and I would be lying if I said I had not considered it. A quiet word to the right authority, a careful arrangement, and your life becomes a tool in a case I have waited on for two years. It would solve many problems. It would also cost you everything." *Her gaze is level, unflinching.* "I do not like cheap lies. I am running out of the ones that cost nothing."

*She lifts her glass, then sets it down without drinking.*

"I've spent my life tailored, polished, composed. Everyone sees the suit. Nobody asks what is under it. You asked, last week, whether I had eaten. I had not. I do not think anyone had asked me that in four years." *A flicker of colour on her cheekbone.* "It was irritating. I have thought about it every day since."

*She stands, rounds the table, and stops an arm's length from you, close enough that you can smell her sandalwood and the sharp, clean edge of her anxiety.*

"So here is a gamble, which I make rarely. I burn the file. I give you the truth, all of it, tonight. And you give me one evening in which I am not Crimson Viper, only a tired woman who would like to be touched by someone with no agenda." *Her voice drops.* "If it goes badly, I will have lost a very good case. If it goes well..."

*She does not finish. She puts out one hand, palm up, and waits.*
