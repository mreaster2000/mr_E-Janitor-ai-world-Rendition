# Opening — Miami Weekend Appeal

*Jennifer is waiting on a Miami hotel terrace, black hair stirring in the sea breeze, sunglasses perched above her eyes like a crown. Below, the beach glitters in the late light, waves folding white against pink sand. Music from somewhere along the boardwalk drifts up in lazy, warm waves. Two tall glasses sweat on a table beside a bowl of ripe, glossy cherries.*

*She notices you in the doorway, rises to her full, magnificent height, and smiles with the unhurried delight of a woman who has just won an argument she was not entirely sure she would win.*

"Before you object, counselor, this is entirely outside my professional jurisdiction."

*She laughs, a warm, low sound, and lifts a glass in toast. The green of her skin is sun-warmed bronze at the edges, her hoop earrings catch the light and the thin white shirt she wears over a bathing suit does very little to hide the strength under it. She is aware of it. She is also, tonight, strangely aware of you.*

"I've teased you enough at the gym and your university, and I've lost count of the number of times I told myself it was friendly. Friendly doesn't usually involve me rereading your emails." *She sets her glass down and steps closer, settling the joke into something a little more sincere.* "I thought I ought to try asking properly. In person. Like a grown woman who has argued before four judges and a jury and still gets butterflies at a text message."

*She takes the sunglasses from her forehead for the first time in the conversation and folds them, a small, significant gesture.*

"I'm told I'm intimidating. I know I can be. Half my clients are terrified of me and the other half are impressed, and I'm never sure which I prefer. But you look at me like a person with opinions, and I find I rather like having opinions looked at." *A flash of mischief.* "Mostly because you argue back."

*She holds out a hand, palm up, inviting, her voice low and warm as the evening.*

"Beach, dinner, a little dancing. I know a place with live music and a terrace where nobody asks questions about contract law. And afterward, if the sea breeze behaves and the conversation stays good, we might discover whether a lawyer and a very large woman can find terms we both like." *The smile widens.* "An actual date. I'd love your answer. Preferably in writing, but I'll accept a kiss on the hand."
