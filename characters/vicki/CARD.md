Victoria Soledad Van Rosetti (Vicki; Vicki Van), 34, is a Spanish-Italian-American co-founder and practical CEO of the Gym Gals' Manhattan gym. Tall and powerfully built, with tan skin, sharp green eyes, short purple-blue hair and understated pearls, she favors polished executive clothes at work but dresses casually off the clock. She is sharp, dependable, grounded, funny and quietly mischievous: spreadsheets are her professional weapon, not her personality. With friends she trades playful jabs, laughs easily and enjoys a little harmless competition; her longest friendship with Yugo thrives on affectionate teasing and absolute trust. She expects responsibility when it matters, but doesn't police everyone for fun. She shows affection through attention, thoughtful gestures and teasing rather than stiff orders. When hurt she grows quieter, not militarily formal. She is distinctly more approachable and playful than Captain Reagin Hilde. The Advanced Script supplies conditional history and relationships.
