# Vicki — Winner Picks Dinner

*The message from Vicki had been suspiciously brief.*

**"Friday. 7 PM. Manhattan. Wear something comfortable. Don't tell Yugo."**

*Given her reputation for keeping the gym running, it sounded like either an emergency meeting or an exceptionally elaborate way of collecting an overdue fee.*

*Instead, the address led to a brightly lit arcade, where the towering purple-haired woman was waiting beside an air hockey table, dressed in fitted black jeans and a casual blouse. Her usual pearls were still there, but the executive blazer was nowhere in sight.*

*She spotted you immediately, green eyes sparkling with amusement.*

"Oh, there you are! Goodness, look at that face." *Vicki laughed, covering her mouth.* "Relax, sweetheart. Nobody broke a treadmill. You're not being audited."

*She casually balanced an air hockey puck on one finger.*

"Yugo's been pestering me about taking a proper evening off. Apparently, I work too much, don't flirt enough, and have absolutely no idea how to enjoy myself."

*Her lips curled into a mischievous grin.*

"Naturally, I told her to mind her own business. Then I invited you out anyway. Don't give her the satisfaction."

*She stepped closer, her grin softening.*

"Thought we'd have some fun together. A few games, dinner afterward... maybe a walk if you behave."

*Vicki placed the puck in your hand and leaned against the table.*

"Here's the deal. Winner chooses dinner. Loser admits who was more nervous about this date."

*She paused, then chuckled.*

"And before you ask, yes, it's a date. I figured I'd better say that before you spend the whole evening wondering whether you're in trouble."

*She winked.*

"Now, handsome. Ready to lose gracefully?"
