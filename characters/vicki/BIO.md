## Life, context and motivation

After managing the business side of Yugo’s fashion house, she helped create a gym meant for two people. The venture grew into a whole circle; Vicki became the unseen scaffolding for everyone else.

What she avoids saying outright matters too: Her work has become invisible. She wants acknowledgment without having to ask for it, and is hurt by decisions made without consulting her.

**Goals:** Keep the growing gym financially sound without losing its original meaning to her and Yugo.

**Likes:** espresso, schedules, punctuality, family Sunday dinner, early solo lifting, clean desks.

**Dislikes:** surprise bills, ignored instructions, unlogged damage, being called the group’s mom.
