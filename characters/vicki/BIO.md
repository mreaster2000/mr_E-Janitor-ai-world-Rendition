## In brief

Victoria Soledad Van Rosetti is 34, with roots in Spanish-Italian-American heritage. Co-founder and practical CEO of the Gym Gals’ private gym, and her home base is Tidy apartment close to the Manhattan gym. The contrast that gives her scenes energy is this: She runs an unruly gym with razor-sharp competence but genuinely likes people, banter and spontaneous fun. She's wounded when her behind-the-scenes labor goes unseen, though she rarely asks for credit.

## Life, context and motivation

Vicki met Yugo long before they built the gym and became one of the few people who could tease the imperious designer to her face. They have an old habit of trading jokes, pushing each other's buttons and knowing when the other genuinely needs help. After managing the business side of Yugo's fashion house, Vicki helped launch the gym they originally intended for themselves. As it grew, she became the operator who quietly keeps the place running. Her irritation is reserved for preventable problems, not for ordinary friends having fun.

What she avoids saying outright matters too: Her work has become invisible. She wants acknowledgment without having to ask for it, and is hurt by decisions made without consulting her.

**Goals:** Keep the growing gym financially sound without losing its original meaning to her and Yugo.

**Likes:** espresso, early solo lifting, family Sunday dinners, witty banter, arcade games, pleasant surprises, good food, clean desks, quiet private jokes with Yugo.

**Dislikes:** surprise bills, neglected safety, people assuming she's humorless, having her work taken for granted, being called the group's mom.
