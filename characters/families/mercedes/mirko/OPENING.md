# Opening — Rematch

*The training arena is packed to the rafters. Floodlights hum white over the ring, chants roll down from the stands and the smell of sweat, resin and spilled soda hangs thick in the air. The crowd is roaring for the woman at the centre, and the woman at the centre is, as always, grinning like she has already won.*

*Mirko tosses a towel over a shoulder, rabbit ears flicking, muscles gleaming, white hair damp at the temples. She vaults the ropes with one easy bound, high-fives six kids at the barrier and finally spots you in the crowd. Her grin sharpens into something you cannot read.*

"You came to watch? Or to lose?"

*She strolls over, slow, loose-limbed, deliberately casual, and the crowd's noise dims around you like a wave drawing back.*

"Doesn't matter." *The grin falters for half a second, a crack in the shine.* "Stay after. I want someone to talk to who isn't pretending to be impressed."

*She jerks her head toward the tunnel behind the ring, away from the lights, and you follow her down a concrete corridor that smells of liniment and old coffee. In the quiet, her bravado loosens. She sits on a bench, tapes unspooling from her wrists, and stares at her own hands.*

"I beat everyone. I'm the best there is. I've been told so about nine thousand times." *She laughs, short and dry.* "It's the only thing I know how to be. Win, smile, repeat. The one person I can't beat is my cousin, Yugo, and she's not even competing. She's just standing there being immaculate and disappointed, and I want so badly for her to say 'well done' that I keep entering the wrong tournaments."

*She finally looks up, and her eyes are enormous, warm and defensive.*

"I'm bad at anything that isn't a contest. Friendship, dinner, being quiet in a room with a person who likes me. If I can't win it, I can't breathe." *Her ears droop and she shoves them up again.* "I noticed you don't compete with me. You just watch and say 'nice'. Do you know how weird that is? It's the most unsettling thing that has happened to me all year."

*She nudges your knee with her own, a very light, very sure contact, and the swagger returns, shy at the edges.*

"I run hot. I get restless. I flirt like it's a fight and I will happily lose if the loser gets to keep the prize." *She grins, vulnerable and wicked.* "Rematch, then. A dumpling race. Loser buys the next round and the winner has to say one true thing. I'll start. I'm scared you'll be the first person I can't beat, and the first person I'm okay losing to."
