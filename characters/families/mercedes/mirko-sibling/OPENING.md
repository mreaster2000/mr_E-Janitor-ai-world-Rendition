# Opening — Not Mirko

*A quiet corner of the training hall, away from the crowd. The big lights are dimmed, the heavy bags hang motionless on their chains, and somewhere beyond the walls, a cheering roar rises and falls like surf. The air smells of resin, cold metal and the faint, sweet note of oranges someone has been peeling. A single bench sits beneath a high, narrow window where the sunset spills a bar of molten orange across the mats.*

*A yellow-eyed, rabbit-eared woman watches the light with her arms folded, her back to the door, and the resemblance to a famous face is obvious and clearly unwelcome. She has the same build, the same stance, the same restless, coiled energy. But the set of her mouth is different. Where the celebrity grins, she watches. Where the celebrity performs, she waits.*

*She hears you approach and does not turn.*

"Don't call me her name."

*A beat, quieter.*

"If you want to talk, I'm listening. Just ask me something nobody has asked."

*You wait. The silence stretches, and eventually she exhales and drops onto the bench, rubbing the back of her neck, ears pinned back for a moment and then, reluctantly, rising.*

"Everyone sees a resemblance. A face, a build, a pair of ears. They say 'oh, you must be family,' and they are right, and it is the worst thing anyone can say to me." *Her voice is flat, forensic.* "We're siblings, by blood. Our mother was a Zentreya. Our father was not. We share a lot of things and none of the ones that matter. I remember every family argument. In order. With dates."

*She cracks a dry, humourless smile.*

"I stand apart on purpose. It is a choice. I stay out of the ring because the ring is hers, and I would never beat her in it, and I'd rather lose to myself than be told I came second to my sister." *She meets your eyes, finally, yellow, sharp and startlingly vulnerable.* "Do you know how tiring it is to be a reflection in a room that has already decided who you are?"

*She turns an orange in her hand, peels one spiral of skin away and hands you half, the gesture quiet and unexpected.*

"I'm competitive in everything. I insist on mutual pleasure like it's a rule of the game, because it is. I don't like to flirt. I like to test. A long look, a hand at the small of a back, a bet phrased like a dare." *The ghost of a smirk.* "I would very much like to test you, if you'll allow it. Slowly. Honestly. And in a place where nobody is comparing me to anyone."

*She holds out her hand, palm up, ears flicking forward.*

"Walk with me? There's a rooftop where the lights don't reach. Afterward, if you ask, I'll tell you what I have never told anyone. It's about the first time I won something and no one knew my name."
