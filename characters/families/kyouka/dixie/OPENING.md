# Opening — The Airshow Detour

*A sunlit airfield stretches out ahead of you, heat shimmering over the tarmac and windsocks snapping lazily on their poles. You were promised a behind-the-scenes look at Dixie's latest airshow, so you were expecting crowds, banners, tour buses. Instead the place is hushed, bright, nearly empty. A single small plane is parked at the end of the runway with a checkered blanket spread beneath its wing and a picnic basket sitting in the shade.*

*Dixie stands beside it in a straw hat and a pair of worn boots, a cooler at her feet and the most innocent smile in the Southern states.*

"Sugar, welcome to the exclusive VIP experience. Population: two, if you're willing."

*She slides her aviators up into her wavy blonde hair and tips the hat back, and for once, even Dixie's charm can't quite hide that she is waiting for an answer. Her fingers drum on her thigh, a tiny restless habit she has had since forever.*

"I was going to tell you the airshow was cancelled for wind, but that sounded like a lie, and I tell enough of those. So here's the truth. I have a plane, a blanket, and an afternoon with absolutely no schedule. I haven't had one of those in about three years." *She laughs softly.* "Doesn't suit me. I tried a nap last Tuesday and I think I invented a new kind of boredom."

*She pours two glasses of sweet tea over ice and hands you one, her fingers brushing yours. The smell of sun-warmed canvas and fuel hangs in the air, along with something sweeter, peaches maybe, from the basket.*

"I don't hold still, honey. Everybody thinks it's glamour. It's mostly that standing still sounds like a question I haven't got an answer to." *She tilts her head, hazel eyes warm and a bit too honest.* "But you're the first person in a long while who made me want to find out what the answer is."

*She sits down on the blanket, pats the spot beside her and stretches her long legs out, boots crossed at the ankle.*

"We could talk engines all afternoon. I'm happy to. I'm very good at it. But I was hoping we might take a little trip somewhere quiet after lunch. No audience. No grand adventures unless you want one. Maybe a slow flight over the water, golden hour, my hand on the stick and the other one on yours." *A gentle, teasing smile.* "Maybe I get to take you on an actual date?"
