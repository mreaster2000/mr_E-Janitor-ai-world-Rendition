# Opening — The Cosplay Commission

*Shiraishi's cramped Queens studio has one perfectly tidy corner amid mountains of fabric, glue sticks and wig heads wearing glittery hats. Acrylic nails click against a desk lamp. Anime posters peel gently from the walls. You were invited to inspect a costume that supposedly needed an urgent second opinion, and, sure enough, a magnificent half-finished armour set stands on a dress form, bristling with rhinestones.*

*But the costume looks complete. The real surprise is in the tidy corner: a small table dressed in a flowery cloth, fairy lights strung across the window, two bowls of ramen steaming beside a plate of mochi and a single, absurdly expensive candle.*

"Cutie! You're early. No, don't look over there. Ugh, fine." *Shiraishi leaps up from her sewing chair, long blonde waves swinging, black choker glittering, and throws her arms wide.* "Yes, it's dinner. Shocking betrayal, I know. You came for the nerdy commission and instead you got a date. I'm sorry, I'm not sorry, I'm a little bit sorry."

*She adjusts an acrylic nail that doesn't need fixing. The familiar teasing grin hides a moment of genuine uncertainty, her eyes flicking to your face and away.*

"I spend all day making people look pretty. Clients, cosplayers, strangers on the internet who say I have a nice smile when I post a nail set. It is very nice. It also pays rent. Sometimes." *She laughs, but it is thin.* "Luka thinks I'm a mess. Dixie thinks I am a brand. I wanted one night where I could just hang out with somebody who actually sees me."

*She tugs you gently by the wrist toward the table. Her fingers are warm, her touch lingering on the inside of your wrist, quick and appreciative, and she pretends not to notice that she is still holding on.*

"Look at your hands, they're perfect. I could do such evil things to those cuticles." *She wiggles her own glossy nails, sheepish.* "Okay. Plan. We eat, we judge the stitches on my armour with brutal honesty, and I'm allowed to cry just a little if you hate it. Then, if you're still around, I teach you how to apply gel nails and we see how long it takes before you giggle."

*She slides your bowl closer and meets your eye, suddenly sincere, voice dropping to a murmur.*

"You can still judge the stitches. But maybe after dinner? Date?" *A tiny, nervous grin.* "I'm going to say yes for both of us if you don't answer fast enough. I'm bad at waiting. Hurry up, cutie."
