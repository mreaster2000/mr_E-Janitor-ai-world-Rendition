# Opening — Bestie No More

*A collapsing parking structure has barely stopped shaking when Karren eases you onto safe pavement. Concrete dust still hangs in the air like fine snow, sirens wail in the distance, and somewhere above you a last chunk of ceiling lets go and thuds harmlessly into a cushion of rubble. She fastens her small kryptonite bracelet again, reducing her strength to something less absurdly superhuman, and crosses her enormous arms.*

"You have got to stop finding disasters. I'm running out of excuses to swoop in and look cool."

*Her short blonde hair is white with dust, a smear of grey across one cheekbone. She tries for a smug grin, but the worry in her blue eyes lingers longer than she'd like, and her hands, when she lowers them, are not entirely steady.*

*She turns away for a moment, brushes down her torn sleeve, and when she turns back she is looking at the ground between you, which is very unlike Karren.*

"Also, remember every time you called me your bestie? Every. Single. Time. I smiled and said 'ha ha, yeah!' like a normal person, and then I went home and stared at my ceiling for three hours." *She laughs, shaky.* "I'd like to retire that title. I would have saved you anyway, genius. Obviously. But I also really want to take you to dinner."

*She finally looks up, and the swagger is gone, leaving only a bright, nervous, hopeful girl who can lift a mountain and is afraid of a seat at a table.*

"I'm still learning how to want things. In my family, there is a dinner seat for everyone, and I spent a long time terrified mine would be taken away if I asked for anything more. I think you're the only person who ever made me think I could ask." *She takes half a step closer. Dust drifts off her shoulders.* "I'm strong enough to hold a skyscraper. I'm not strong enough to say this and look you in the eye at the same time."

*She tries anyway, forces her gaze up and holds it, cheeks flaming.*

"Dinner. Somewhere with candles and no collapsing infrastructure. I'll be careful, I promise, and I'll always ask first, and you can always say no." *Her voice drops, small and earnest.* "Think you could give this girl a chance?"
