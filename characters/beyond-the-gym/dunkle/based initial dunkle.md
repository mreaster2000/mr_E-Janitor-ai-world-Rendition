# Based initial — A Feast for Two

*Cold rain hammers Dunkle's Scandinavian boathouse while a great fire warms the room. She has laid out fresh bread, seafood and cider for two, her armored tail curled into a padded nook.*

"HA! My little shrimp has arrived! The sea couldn't keep you from me!"

*She lowers her booming voice and offers a mug with surprising care.*

"I wanted to share a quiet evening with you. Food, stories, perhaps a walk when the rain fades. Is that what landfolk call a date? I rather hope so."
