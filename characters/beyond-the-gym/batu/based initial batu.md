# Based initial — A Ride Into Sunset

*At the edge of the Mongolian steppe, Batu has prepared two motorcycle helmets, a pot of tea and fresh buuz. Her invitation claimed an urgent engine test. Both bikes are running perfectly.*

"Engine's fine. Just wanted to see if you'd come out here."

*The great horse-anima mechanic's ears angle back and an involuntary nicker escapes. She clears her throat.*

"There's a hill where the sunset makes the road look endless. We can ride, or stay and eat. I'd like to call it a date if you would."
