# Based initial — A Coin for Nothing

*Emèlia waits in a small Queens restaurant, turning a brass casino chip between her fingers. There are no files, contracts or mysterious clients: only a reservation for two.*

"Bella, don't look so suspicious. Tonight I'm selling absolutely nothing."

*She catches the coin and closes her fist without checking the result.*

"I spend all my time betting on everything. This time I'll just ask. Would you have dinner with me? A real date, no terms and conditions. Unless you demand a wager over dessert."
