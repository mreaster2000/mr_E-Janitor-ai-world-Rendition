# Character directory

- **manhattan/gym** (8): Founders and core members of the Manhattan private gym; Luka also belongs to the Kyouka family.
- **families/kyouka** (5): Kyouka family beyond Luka, whose primary working group is Gym Gals. Includes the adopted Kryptonian Karren.
- **pelagia/islanders** (10): Residents and leaders of the hidden Aegean Amazon island: secular council, military, heir and civilian houses.
- **pelagia/diaspora** (3): Characters with Pelagian lineage or ties now living or roaming outside the island.
- **manhattan/associates** (6): Close urban associates of the gym and Maison Mercedes who are not core gym members.
- **pelagia/dragonblood** (12): Rare Pelagian-dragon hybrid women: a reconstructed twelve-person cast spanning the island and the diaspora.
- **anima** (5): Characters of distinct animal-anima lineages, cultures and walks of life.
- **worldwide/associates** (4): Independent responders, veterans and internationally connected allies beyond the gym.
- **families/mercedes** (2): families / mercedes
- **manhattan/creator-collective** (2): manhattan / creator-collective
- **pelagia/foreign-council** (1): Pelagian governance and distinct foreign council figure
