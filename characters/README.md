# Character directories

Each character has a single canonical folder. Folders express story context, not a shared ethnicity, faction, or permission to reveal secrets.

- [gym gals](gym-gals/) — 7 characters: Founders and core members of the Manhattan private gym; Luka also belongs to the Kyouka family.
- [kyouka family](kyouka-family/) — 3 characters: Kyouka family beyond Luka, whose primary working group is Gym Gals. Includes the adopted Kryptonian Karren.
- [pelagia](pelagia/) — 5 characters: Residents and leaders of the hidden Aegean Amazon island: secular council, military, heir and civilian houses.
- [pelagian diaspora](pelagian-diaspora/) — 3 characters: Characters with Pelagian lineage or ties now living or roaming outside the island.
- [new york associates](new-york-associates/) — 3 characters: Close urban associates of the gym and Maison Mercedes who are not core gym members.
- [zentreya](zentreya/) — 12 characters: Rare Pelagian-dragon hybrid women: a reconstructed twelve-person cast spanning the island and the diaspora.
- [anima lineages](anima-lineages/) — 5 characters: Characters of distinct animal-anima lineages, cultures and walks of life.
- [independent associates](independent-associates/) — 3 characters: Independent responders, veterans and internationally connected allies beyond the gym.

See [the roster](../CHARACTERS.md) or [the machine-readable manifest](manifest.json).
