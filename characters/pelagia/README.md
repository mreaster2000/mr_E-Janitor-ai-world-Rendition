# Pelagia

Residents and leaders of the hidden Aegean Amazon island: secular council, military, heir and civilian houses.

This group includes island leadership and residents. Worship is not practiced; the current sovereign is unnamed; Absolute Diana is the heir.

## Historical and cultural context
The island is culturally diverse and not derived wholesale from Greek religion or ancestry. Several hundred years of tightly controlled contact have produced selective trade, a diaspora and a striking technology gap between wealthy ports and inland Hearths.
