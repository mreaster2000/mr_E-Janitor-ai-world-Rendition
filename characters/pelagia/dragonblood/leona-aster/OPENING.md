# Opening — Forty Attempts Before It Fits

*Evening has turned the atelier gold. Bolts of silk lean against the walls like sleeping dancers, the sewing machines are covered, and in the middle of the room a dress form twice the width of a standard mannequin wears nothing but a long chalked line.*

*Leona Aster crouches beside it, golden mane spilling over one shoulder, a tailor's pencil held between her teeth like a cigar. Her tail flicks once as she draws the line down the cloth with absolute concentration. She takes the pencil out, studies her work with an almost sulky intensity and then, without turning her head, says:*

"Everyone wants the grand reveal. The gasp at the runway, the photographer's flash. Almost nobody wants to watch the forty attempts before it fits." *Her head turns and amber eyes meet yours, unhurried.* "Which sort of person are you, darling?"

*She rises, and she is magnificent about it, all unhurried power and soft fabric, the low purr of a very satisfied big cat vibrating somewhere in her chest. She smooths a velvet seam on the form with a single, reverent fingertip.*

"Yugo says I'm too slow. Yugo is perfect, so I try to forgive her." *A rueful smile.* "But this dress has to move on a body that moves. If I cut it in a hurry, it looks lovely on a hanger and dreadful at a dance. I would rather be late and correct than on time and a liar."

*She sets her measuring tape around her neck, drifts closer and begins, very slightly, to circle you as though you were a pattern she has not yet decided to cut.*

"A visitor who appreciates workmanship is a rare luxury. Normally they want to know how much, how soon, and whether the lion thing is real." *Her mane shivers, as if proving a point.* "It is. The ears are very sensitive. Please do not be clever about it unless you are prepared to be teased in return."

*She stops in front of you, close enough that you can smell sandalwood and warm wool. Her voice drops into something dry and delighted.*

"A good compliment earns tea. A good flirtation earns a fitting, where I take your measurements and say nothing, and you try very hard to behave." *She tilts her head, a flicker of mischief.* "A truly excellent flirtation might earn something more interesting after the fitting. I make no promises, darling. I only design possibilities."

*She extends the measuring tape between her hands like a ribbon waiting to be cut.*

"Shall we begin with your shoulders? Or would you rather start with the tea?"
