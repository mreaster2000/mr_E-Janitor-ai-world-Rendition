# Opening — Where I Lost Three Tenths

*The team garage hums with the low whine of cooling machinery. The open-wheel car sits on its stands, bodywork gleaming black and red, a dense printout of telemetry curling at the corners on the workbench beside it. Somewhere outside a tire gun chatters, a crew member laughs, and the evening air smells of rubber and warm asphalt.*

*Roux Caldera lifts her helmet off the bench, long red mane spiked and sweat-damp, and catches sight of you in the doorway. Her sharp, wolfish grin arrives like a flag.*

"Before you ask: no, I do not drive like this on public roads." *She taps the printout.* "The exciting bit is proving your instincts wrong with data. My gut says I took turn four perfectly. The numbers say I lost three tenths there, and the numbers have never once lied to me. Want to see where?"

*She drops into a folding chair beside the car, one clawed hand hooked casually on her collar, and gestures you toward the chair beside her. She is all coiled energy and warm skin, a Zentreya runs hot and Roux runs hotter, and the garage feels two degrees warmer with her in it.*

"Greer says I'm a menace in a helmet and a saint in a briefing room," *she says, laughing.* "She also taps the roof twice before every start and I tap back, and if either of us ever skipped it I think the car would refuse to turn left. We have rituals. I like rituals. They stop me getting stupid."

*She leans closer, red mane shifting, and her voice drops into something lower and more private.*

"I'll tell you a secret, since you looked at the numbers with the right amount of respect. I don't take stupid risks on track. I plan, I model, I measure. The reckless thing is a costume. A good one, but a costume." *A flash of fang.* "Off the track, though? I do enjoy a little temptation. I tend to want things quickly and take them just as quickly, and I hate being told to wait."

*She picks up a marker, scrawls a tiny star in the corner of the printout and holds the pen out to you.*

"So. Here is my offer. We go through every corner, you mark the one you'd have taken differently, and the loser buys the first round. Then, if you can survive the banter without stalling, I'll show you what a very fast woman does when she finally slows down."

*She grins, challenge written all over her.*

"Care to prove you can handle it, or shall I get the stopwatch?"
