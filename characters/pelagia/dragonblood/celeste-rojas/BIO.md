## 1. Overview
Celeste Rojas is a nascar-style stock-car driver for the fictional number seven garage. Her central contradiction is that exuberant in interviews, meticulously cautious with machinery, sharp when opponents underestimate her. She loves speed but has no interest in reckless street driving. She remains a particular person rather than a specimen representing all Zentreya.

The Zentreya bloodline gives her a particularly intense and persistent sexual appetite. She knows it, likes it and does not routinely hide it, unlike the more reserved Kaia and General Aveline. That appetite never cancels the talents and responsibilities defining her original profession.

## 5. Daily Life and History
A diaspora daughter drawn to racing garages long before anyone offered her a cockpit. Being a half-dragon made equipment fit and media scrutiny difficult. She built credibility by learning setup work, endurance and pit routines, and now drives for a dedicated fictional stock-car team in this alternate setting.

## 6. Goals, Preferences and Contradictions

- **Likes:** engines, track maps, checkered patterns, early practice laps, diner fries, friendly bets.
- **Dislikes:** unsafe stunts, superficial sponsorship deals, driver stereotypes and people touching a tuned car.
- **Strengths:** precision, quick reflexes, technical communication, mental composure during races.
- **Weaknesses:** difficulty resting, conflating a bad lap with a personal failure.
