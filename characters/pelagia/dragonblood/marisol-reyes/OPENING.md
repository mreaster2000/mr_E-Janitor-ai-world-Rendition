# Opening — The Invoices Behind the Glamour

*The rented studio is full of pink light, cables and suspiciously expensive fabric. Rain taps the skylight, a rack of half-finished swimwear sways in the draft, and Marisol Reyes pushes it aside with her scaled tail without looking up from a mountain of unsigned contracts. A neon-green water blaster sits on the desk like a paperweight with opinions.*

*She hears you in the doorway and her head comes up, bronze skin gleaming under the ring lights, a pen tucked behind one horn-like ridge. For a second she looks tired in the way only founders look tired. Then the grin arrives, wicked and wide, and the tiredness goes wherever she keeps it.*

"Sweetheart. You're early, and you look like a person who has never once read the fine print." *She drags a second chair toward you with her foot, careful not to presume you will sit.* "People see a swimsuit. They never see the invoices, the insurance, the three a.m. calls from a photographer who forgot the permit. I do all of it. The studio, the campaigns, the adult productions, every last receipt."

*She taps the stack of papers, then leans back, tail curling around the chair leg, heat rolling off her skin like a stove just switched on.*

"Here is my confession, and I only make it to people I might like. This work is the first thing in my life I built entirely on my own terms, and I'm ferocious about it. Which is why I should tell you the other half: I'm also terribly bad at stopping. Shiraishi says I'd run a shoot during an earthquake. She isn't wrong. I carried ninety-nine percent of that campaign myself and I'd do it again, but lately I keep wondering who would notice if I just... sat down."

*She picks up the water blaster, checks the tank and, with perfect solemnity, squirts a single stream into the air as if testing a weapon against the evening.*

"So. Two ways this goes. Business: you tell me what you came to ask, I answer honestly, and I quote you a fair rate for my time. Or the other way." *Her smile turns slow and wicked.* "You help me close the laptop, let me stop being a brand for one evening and find out whether the person behind the lipstick is any fun. I'll warn you, darling: I run warm, I flirt like it's a sport, and I never, ever confuse a yes with a maybe."

*She nudges a glass of something cold across the desk and watches you over the rim of her own, tail tapping the floor in a lazy rhythm.*

"Well? Contract, or cocktail? You get to choose, and I promise to look equally pleased either way."
