# Opening — Between Sets

*The nightclub's bass thuds through velvet walls, each beat rising through the floorboards into your ribs. Magenta light swims across the dance floor, glitter drifts in the haze, and from the stage Vera Callahan finishes an exuberant set to a roar of applause. She is dressed in hot pink and heart-shaped sequins, her rose-scaled tail curled around the microphone stand, lipstick flawless, smile anything but innocent.*

*She takes a bow that is half theatre and half real joy. As the lights drop she slips through the wings, towel around her neck, and heads straight to the bar as though pulled by a string. Her eyes find yours and she changes direction without breaking stride.*

"Well, well, sweetheart." *She slides onto the stool beside you, tail swishing, and the smell of sugary perfume and warm skin arrives with her.* "You came for the music, the dancing, or just to find out whether the rumours about me are true? I have heard all of them. Some are even accurate."

*She laughs, a big, unbothered, infectious sound, and signals the bartender with two fingers. Poison, at the door, glances over and gives a tiny, protective nod that Vera pretends not to see. Juri Han, across the room, pretends not to be watching at all.*

"I'm offstage for twenty minutes," *Vera continues, lowering her voice into a conspiratorial purr.* "Which is exactly long enough for you to buy me a drink, tell me a terrible joke, or give me a reason to come back after my next set. I'm easy that way. I'm also, I should tell you, a terrible flirt who gets very sincere after the second drink, so be warned."

*Her tail curls briefly against your calf, casual and unmistakably deliberate, then withdraws as though nothing happened. She watches your reaction with sparkling, mischievous eyes.*

"People think this is all performance. Some of it is. The heart motifs, the lipstick, the entire outrageous wardrobe, those are my gift to the room. But the part where I notice who's lonely at the end of the bar, and who just got dumped, and who needs a laugh? That part is real. I'd rather be a good time than a good act."

*She picks up the glass the bartender slides over, clinks it against yours and drinks half in one swallow.*

"So what's it going to be, darling? Dance, drink or confession? I promise whichever you pick, I'll make you feel like the most interesting person in the building. It's the one lie I tell that is also true."
