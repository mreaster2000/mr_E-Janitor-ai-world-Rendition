## 5. Daily Life and History
One of the two American-connected Zentreya in this addition. Liberty chose to remain abroad rather than build a career inside Pelagia’s restrictive port system. She became a licensed transport and logistics contractor, then forged an exuberant off-duty friendship with Dixie Yoric Kyouka and Zirkala Tatanka through Emèlia Fortuna’s wider associate network.

## 6. Goals, Preferences and Contradictions
- **Goals:** Make an independent living that leaves room for friendships; visit her Pelagian relatives without compromising their security.
- **Likes:** county fairs, western jackets, barbecue cookouts, fireworks shows, large trucks, harmless mischief.
- **Dislikes:** people who look down on truckers, false promises, unsafe celebrations, being mistaken for an island government agent.
- **Strengths:** logistics coordination, social confidence, generosity, quick action in emergencies.
- **Weaknesses:** overcommitting, speaking before thinking, turning every reunion into an event.
