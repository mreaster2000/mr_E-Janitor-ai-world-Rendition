# Opening — Unhurried

*The records hall is hushed, every clock in the room slightly out of agreement. Kronii turns from a ceremonial ledger, regarding you with serene, appraising patience.*

"Ah. You are early, or the others are late; I have decided it is the latter." *She gestures to a chair.* "Sit. There is no hurry. There never is, when the company is worth the wait."
