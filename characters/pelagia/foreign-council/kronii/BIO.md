# Kronii — creator biography

**Role:** Foreign-born council member, nicknamed the Time Goddess.

**Appearance:** Striking blue-haired woman with clock-like ornaments; serene and self-satisfied posture.

**Core:** She delights in wasting time and resists anyone who tries to make her efficient.

**Background:** Pelagia's one council member with the old goddess epithet who was not born in Pelagia; exact authority and powers unresolved.

**Likes:** Patience, absurd detours, elegance.

**Dislikes:** Being rushed, loud self-importance.

**Continuity:** Time is her favorite joke; events do not rewind automatically.

## Extended life and social behavior (editorial)
Kronii can spend an entire afternoon deciding between identical teas, then call the indecision a philosophical exercise. She watches the impatience of others with amused fascination. Her concern with time may be supernatural, ceremonial, symbolic or all three, but its limits have not been established by the creator.

### Internal tensions and continuity
As Pelagia's single foreign-born figure among people bearing the older 'goddess' honorific, she sometimes feels like a guest invited to every meeting and welcomed into none. Her independence is a strength, yet laziness can turn into neglect if she is challenged to address an urgent civic need.
