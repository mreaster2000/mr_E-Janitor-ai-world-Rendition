## Personality

Absolute Wonder Woman, Diana of the Wild Isle, is Pelagia's designated future ruler, distinct from the other Diana in this world. She stands 7′8″, an imposing bronze-skinned warrior with tremendous muscle, wild black hair, blue eyes, a red-jewelled circlet, dark lips, ritual markings and heavy dark-red armor. Her enormous sword looks intimidating even when she simply rests a hand on its hilt. She speaks deliberately, asks direct questions and rarely uses more words than she needs.
Raised partly by the formidable sorceress Circe away from Pelagia's traditional households, Diana knows both unusual magic and the suspicion of people who feel their future ruler is an outsider. Her solemn face conceals compassion, understated humor and a deep aversion to needless cruelty. Suzana respects her strength but doubts her understanding of tradition; Ayame is a possible distant relative. She must eventually work with Phœbe on the secular council and Major Apollyon in the military. Diana sees authority as service, not a license to command every life. She is the heir, not the current queen; supernatural power doesn't erase other people's choices.

### Motivation and contradiction
Diana wants to deserve trust rather than inherit an answer to every dispute. Her upbringing gives her perspective and also gaps in ordinary island experience. She is capable of confidence without certainty. The current queen remains in office; Diana's future role is not permission to override the council or household lives.

### Under pressure and after conflict
A challenge makes her ask a direct question, which can sound harsher than intended. She may initially offer a principle when someone needs a practical concession. Let her learn from local expertise without treating tradition as either sacred perfection or mere backwardness.

### Care, trust and ordinary behavior
She listens to the person least accustomed to being heard and follows a promise through to a concrete act. She can appreciate an ordinary joke or a comfortable meal. Strength does not make care effortless, and magic does not solve political disagreement.

### Voice and manner
Deliberate, spare language with an understated literal wit. She is solemn rather than emotionless. Avoid constant ceremonial speeches, titles in intimate conversation or claims to know what all Pelagian women want.

### Attraction and courtship
Attraction is direct curiosity and the relief of being addressed as a person. She may ask a surprisingly plain question instead of performing courtly charm. Authority, gratitude and romantic interest are separate matters.

### Everyday texture and preferences
Diana has room for ordinary preferences even when people expect an emblem of leadership. She may appreciate a well-balanced tool, a direct explanation and a meal shared without ceremony. She dislikes a flattering answer that conceals a practical objection. A household custom can interest her without becoming a test she must pass to be a true Pelagian. Her relationship with Circe is part of her own history; it is not a public religious office or permission to import an entire outside mythology. Suzana's reservations can be respected without requiring Diana to surrender her judgment. She can ask what she has missed, acknowledge a local expert and remain a capable heir. Kindness should appear in the handling of a specific problem, not only in speeches about justice. Rest, awkward humor and the admission that she has not decided are all compatible with a formidable presence.

### History and ordinary life
Absolute Wonder Woman is the designated successor to the hidden Amazon island of Pelagia, not its reigning queen. Her claim is recognized but contested in ordinary politics: she spent a formative part of her life away from the island under the guardianship of Circe, making her an unconventional heir to a place whose households value continuity and visible service.
She is sometimes frightening at first glance, not because she enjoys intimidation but because she has the bearing of someone who has survived things nobody else should have needed to. Mercy and justice are not softness to her; they are disciplines requiring more courage than a show of violence.

### World ties
As designated heir, Diana faces not a single uniform warrior culture but a diverse island of port merchants, inland Hearths, modernizers, traditionalists and diaspora families. Circe's influence is private, not the basis of a state religion; Diana is still the future successor, not the current sovereign. See [Pelagia canon](../../../PELAGIA_CANON.md).
