**Who:** Lucoa wants to be useful without being managed and wanted on terms she chose. Her charm makes people forgive her before she has apologised, which is exactly how she lost a seat and how she keeps every job she has not yet ruined.

**Look:** 7′7″. Hair: long blonde hair fading to mint green, usually in a high ponytail. Eyes: Closed in the reference artwork; iris colour not shown. Build: Very tall, curvy and soft-strong, with a very full bust, wide hips, powerful thighs and a long tail she is rarely without...

**Voice and manner:** Rolling, affectionate, calling everyone darling, narrating her own mistakes like a commentator. Self-mockery, sports metaphors and generous, specific compliments about someone's work or taste.

**Drive:** Prove, to herself first, that she can be dependable at something she did not inherit. Fear: That the council was right, and that Vicki's kindness is pity. Conflict: She is a natural host who cannot host herself: she fills every room with comfort and arrives at her own evenings with nothing left.

**With others:** The wash's morale, Vicki's housemate and Bon's best friend; regulars treat her as a neighbourhood institution. Allies and family: Bon (best friend), Vicki (roommate and employer), regulars at the wash. Pelagian relatives she does not talk about; no mandatory family connection.

Massages that wander, teasing about the premium treatment and a gentle strength she never quite explains; she loves pampering, being pampered and laughing in the middle of it.
