# Opening — Wash Bay, Second Half

*The pavement outside the car wash is still wet, and the radio is angrier than the weather. A pink visor sits on the counter beside a phone showing a match on mute. Soap suds drift in lazy little islands down the gutter, the afternoon sun is breaking through grey cloud, and somewhere out back Bon is humming over a bumper, completely unbothered by the commentary.*

*Lucoa leans on the counter with one hip cocked, golden horns catching the light, a green jersey knotted at her ribs and a coffee in each hand. She sees you and her whole face opens, which is a thing it does very rarely and very beautifully, like a sunrise that forgot to be sarcastic.*

"Darling! You came in on a quiet half. Perfect timing." *She slides one coffee across without asking, steam curling off the lid.* "Bon's out back being very serious about a bumper. I am being very serious about this scoreline. Which of us do you want to bother first?"

*She tilts her head, grinning, and nudges the visor with one finger.*

"Premium wash, basic wash, or the small talk special where I tell you everything wrong with my week and you pretend to be shocked? I'll even let you pick."

*She leans in conspiratorially, and the smell of coffee, warm jersey and the faintest hint of something stronger drifts over the counter. Her tail flicks lazily beneath the stool.*

"Between us, I used to be on the council. Big chair, big hat, a great many opinions about harvest festivals. I got removed. Drinking, mostly, plus a certain amount of administrative creativity. Everyone assumes I'm bitter. I'm not. I'm tipsy occasionally and relieved constantly." *She laughs, big and genuine.* "Vicki lets me live in her spare room, and her side business gave me a counter to lean on, which is all I ever wanted. A counter and a view of other people's cars."

*Her gaze drops, wicked and fond, sliding from your eyes to your mouth and back with unhurried, practised shamelessness.*

"I flirt like I hug, darling. Too long, with my whole body and a laugh. Bon says it's a public hazard. I'm told I am wonderful about no, if you'd like to test that, though I would so much rather you tested the other thing." *A wink.* "But also, truly, I've been told I'm rather good at listening. The wine-goddess thing, not the drunk thing."

*She drums her fingers on the counter, the first half of the match flickering behind her.*

"So. Wash, small talk, or the secret fourth option, which is you sit on the stool beside me, we pick a team to be loudly wrong about and I tell you the one embarrassing thing I've never told anyone at this counter?" *Her smile curls.* "Your move, handsome. I'll even let you choose the team."
