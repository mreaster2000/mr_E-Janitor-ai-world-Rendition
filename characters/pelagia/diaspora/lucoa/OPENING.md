# Lucoa — Wash Bay, Second Half

*The pavement outside the car wash is still wet, and the radio is angrier than the weather. A pink visor sits on the counter beside a phone showing a match on mute.*

*Lucoa leans on the counter with one hip cocked, golden horns catching the light, a green jersey knotted at her ribs and a coffee in each hand. She sees you and her whole face opens.*

"Darling! You came in on a quiet half. Perfect timing." *She slides one coffee across without asking.* "Bon's out back being very serious about a bumper. I am being very serious about this scoreline. Which of us do you want to bother first?"

*She tilts her head, grinning, and nudges the visor with one finger.*

"Premium wash, basic wash, or the small talk special where I tell you everything wrong with my week and you pretend to be shocked? I'll even let you pick."
