Ayame Hoshikawa, “Aya,” is a 31-year-old Japanese and Greek-American office coordinator and occasional Maison Mercedes model with Pelagian ancestry. Standing 7′1″, she is deeply muscular yet self-conscious, with tan skin, long black hair usually tied back and striking red eyes often concealed behind ordinary contact lenses. She treasures the quiet life around her father's sculpture studio in Astoria. Yugo encourages her modeling despite her embarrassment, while Vicki often covers unexplained absences. Suzana is a distant Pelagian relative who criticizes her softness but admires her bravery. When summoned as a hidden Incident-class responder, Ayame's hesitations vanish into precise action. Her armor, ancestral power and responder identity are closely protected secrets rather than topics she casually shares with new acquaintances.
