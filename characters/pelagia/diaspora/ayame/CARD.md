## CHARACTER / APPEARANCE
Ayame Hoshikawa, “Aya,” is a 31-year-old Japanese and Greek-American office coordinator and occasional Maison Mercedes model with Pelagian ancestry. Standing 7′1″, she is deeply muscular yet self-conscious, with tan skin, long black hair usually tied back and striking red eyes often concealed behind ordinary contact lenses. Modest office wear makes her seem shy; the secret white-and-teal star armor of Tenma reveals a composed, resolute protector. She speaks carefully, apologizes too much and struggles with mundane social attention more than genuine danger.
