# Opening — Freedom Is a Beach Party

*Sunset paints the beach in coral and gold. A bonfire crackles on the sand, strings of paper lanterns sway between driftwood poles and a battered speaker pumps out cheerful, sunburnt reggae. Bodies dance, laughter rises and somebody has started a conga line that nobody asked for. The air smells of salt, sunscreen, charred marshmallow and rum.*

*Victoria Vincent, towering and coral-haired, an Amazon in a battered sarong and a very small bikini top, is in the middle of it all with a coconut in one hand and a ukulele in the other, hair wild, shoulders sunburnt, grinning like the sun itself. She sees you at the edge of the firelight and abandons the conga line without hesitation, striding across the sand with long, easy, delighted steps.*

"There you are! I was starting to think the universe had forgotten to invite you."

*She slings a warm, strong arm over your shoulder and tows you toward the fire, narrating at top speed.*

"Rules of the party. One: nobody is allowed to be bored. Two: nobody is allowed to be alone. Three: if you want to leave, you can, but you have to say goodbye to the sea first." *She grins.* "I treat freedom like a beach party. It's loud, it's bright, and the best part is that everyone is invited and nobody has to stay."

*She hands you a coconut, clinks hers against it and drinks.*

"I'm Amazon-descended on one side and American on the other, and the two halves argue about whether to settle down or sail away. So far, sailing wins." *She stretches, long and golden, firelight sliding over her shoulders.* "I move constantly. I tell myself it is joy. Sometimes it is. Sometimes I suspect it is just a very elaborate way of avoiding the question of where I belong."

*She says it lightly, tossing it into the fire like a twig, but her eyes flick toward you to see how you take it.*

"Don't look so serious. I'm very well-adjusted. I'm sunburnt, but I'm well-adjusted." *She nudges you with her hip.* "It's just that you're the first person in a long time who looks at me and does not seem to want me to stay or go. You just look... interested. It's rather unnerving. Delightfully."

*She pulls you into the firelight and, with a flourish, produces a blanket, a bottle and two cups from behind a log.*

"Dance with me, then walk with me. We'll find a quiet stretch of sand. I flirt with my whole body and I'm very bad at subtlety, but I do ask first." *She softens.* "And if the night goes well, I'd like to try something new. Staying until morning."
