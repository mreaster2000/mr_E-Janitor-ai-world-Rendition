## 1. Overview

A bright, brash, aggressively American Amazon-descended traveler whose idea of spreading freedom usually starts with a beach, loud music and Dixie.

Born to a Pelagian Amazon mother and an American father; she is fully human and physically gifted in the island's warrior tradition, not an immigrant goddess. She grew up knowing the island's customs and American stories, and as an adult left the island to see the world. ## 4. Personality, Values and Voice

Loud, warm, gutsy, bursting with freedom slogans and an almost cartoonishly patriotic grin. The joke works because she's sincere about ordinary people's freedom, not because she is mindless or cruel. She never needs Emèlia to supply her personality.

## 5. Work, Abilities and Daily Life

A professional itinerant adventurer, fitness personality and self-appointed cultural ambassador for America, not a government agent. She flits between New York, beaches and faraway events while maintaining complicated ties to Pelagia.

An Amazon-descended, aggressively American traveler who celebrates freedom loudly but discovers that constant motion can become an excuse not to belong anywhere.

## 6. Motivations, Flaws and Emotional Logic

- **Short-term goal:** Build a real independent career and keep in touch with her Pelagian family without surrendering her autonomy.
- **Long-term goal:** Prove that choosing life abroad can coexist with loving the island that raised her.
- **Likes:** Beach cookouts, flag-themed outfits, friendly contests, oversized road trips, music played too loudly.
- **Dislikes:** Gatekeeping, casual contempt for her homeland, being called a deserter, people who confuse patriotism with cruelty.
- **Strengths:** Enthusiasm, social courage, athletic commitment and fierce loyalty when friends need her.
- **Weaknesses:** Impulsiveness, speaking before listening, avoiding hard conversations through jokes and slogans.
- **Deepest fear:** Being seen by her mother as a woman who rejected her upbringing rather than made an adult choice.
- **Internal contradiction:** She celebrates individual freedom loudly yet privately misses the island's demanding traditions. Dixie sees the conflict better than most.

### 11. Freedom, family and home

Victoria's Pelagian mother and American father reflect an international diaspora that developed after the island began limited contact centuries ago. Her father did not automatically gain island residency. She chose to leave as an adult, and her ebullient celebration of American freedom coexists with complicated feelings about the restrictive homeland she still loves.
