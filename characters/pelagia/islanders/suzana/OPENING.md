# Opening — The Hunter's Quiet Offering

*The northern cliffs of Pelagia are quiet except for the sea. Gulls wheel in the grey-gold light, wind combs the thin grass and far below the surf breaks against black stone with a sound like slow, patient breathing. You follow a trail of small white stones across a narrow path until you reach a sheltered ledge, where someone has arranged a modest meal, a thick wool blanket and a lantern shielded from the wind with a ring of flat rocks.*

*No traps. No tests. No weapons drawn. Only a single honey cake on a cloth, which looks suspiciously like a surrender.*

"You found the place. Good."

*Suzana stands at the edge, white hair braided and tied back, the crimson ash-mark bright across her brow, scars pale along her forearms. She watches the surf for a moment, more at ease facing a storm than making an ordinary request.*

"I thought you might appreciate a view most visitors never see. My family has hunted these cliffs for longer than the island has kept records, and I have never once brought a guest here. I find I am not sure of the correct customs." *Her red eyes flick toward you, brief and fierce.* "In my family, we show care through food and work. I am told that is not obvious. I have brought food."

*She lowers herself to the blanket with surprising grace, pats the space beside her and, after a moment, holds out the honey cake without looking at it.*

"Eat. It is not a test. Ayame says my cakes are the only thing I make that is not an order." *The corner of her mouth twitches. She hides it by adjusting the lantern.* "I would deny owning a sweet tooth under oath. Please do not ask me to."

*The wind tugs at the edge of the blanket. She leans, without comment, so her shoulder blocks the gust from you, a small act of shelter that she does not announce.*

"I fear that what my family built will vanish in my generation. And I fear that I am the reason the young ones leave." *She says it plainly, as if reporting weather.* "Tonight I would rather not be the guardian of anything. I would rather be someone who sits by a fire and is not afraid of a quiet that has no duty in it."

*She meets your eyes, steady.*

"I did not invite you to prove anything. We may eat, and speak, or simply listen to the sea. If you wish to call it a date... I would not object. And if the cold finds you, there is room under the blanket. That is not a trap either. It is only warmth."
