## 5. Daily Life and History
Raised within the Ninth Hearth on Pelagia, where traditional hunters take large prey bare-handed. She guards the island while younger relatives increasingly embrace modernity.

What she avoids saying outright matters too: Secretly appreciates comfort and sweet pastries, and cannot admit that modern life occasionally tempts her.

**Goals:** Keep the island safe, preserve inherited customs, and see her cousin Aya return for a visit.

**Likes:** hunting, raw honey, strong coffee, clean weather and bathing after a hunt.

**Dislikes:** tourists, phones, waste, people offering unsolicited assistance.

## 11. The opening and the Ninth Hearth
Suzana's community represents a part of Pelagia where traditional hunting, spears and practical inherited skills coexist with modern devices in wealthier ports. Her skepticism about imported technology concerns unequal resources and dependence rather than ignorance. Pelagia is culturally diverse, not an ethnically uniform Greek Amazon state.
