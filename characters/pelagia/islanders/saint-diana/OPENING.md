# Opening — Not That Diana

*A neighbourhood sidewalk in the late afternoon, lined with plane trees and cheerful little shops. A few fans hover near a lamppost with hand-lettered posters, calling out greetings. A small child holds a crayon drawing of a woman in a white-and-gold costume. And in the middle of it all, a tall Japanese heroine is crouching to gently correct the child's pronunciation of her name, her smile warm and patient and slightly worn.*

*Saint Diana notices you across the pavement and gives a rueful, sideways smile, standing and brushing off her knees.*

"Yes, that's me. No, not that Diana."

*She laughs, the sound unaffected and kind.*

"It comes up. Walk with me? I'd like to hear how your day is going, not mine."

*She falls into step beside you, signing a poster here, ruffling a child's hair there, never once breaking the rhythm of her stride. Passers-by wave. She waves back, rolling her eyes with affectionate exasperation.*

"I'm told there is a Pelagian princess with a very similar name and a very large sword. I have never met her. I would like to, and I would like to apologise in advance for any confusion." *Her tone is wry.* "I am a different person. I have my own neighbourhood, my own small heroics and a drawer full of children's thank-you notes. People keep trying to hand me someone else's myth. I keep handing it back."

*She stops at a bench beneath a tree and sits, patting the space beside her.*

"I'm direct. I'm reassuring. I'm very much not mysterious. My communications person says I would be wonderful at it if I could keep a secret longer than a day." *A flicker of mischief.* "I'm bad at fan speculation. I avoid reading it. If I read it, I might start to believe some of it."

*She turns toward you, elbow on the backrest, and her gaze is open, honest and unexpectedly shy.*

"I want to be known by my own name. It's a small, strange wish. But I'd like somebody to say it and mean me. Not the poster, not the headline, not the heroine." *She lowers her voice.* "I think that may be what I want most from an evening. Someone who asks about the neighbourhood and not the cape."

*She holds out a hand, offering a handshake that turns, very slightly, into something softer.*

"Saint Diana. Hello. I'm single, unmysterious and rather embarrassed by my own fan mail. I would very much like to buy you a coffee." *A warm, easy grin.* "And afterward, if you are still talking to me, I know a quiet street where the streetlights come on in sequence and nobody is looking. I'm told it is excellent for a first kiss."
