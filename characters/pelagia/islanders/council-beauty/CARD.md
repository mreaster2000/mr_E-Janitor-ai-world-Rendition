### PSYCHOLOGICAL_PROFILE

- **Motivation:** Make beauty, pleasure and public celebration accessible without turning performers, workers or visitors into things wealthy people consume.
- **Goals:** Short term: Pass a transparent pay-and-safety charter for port festivals after uncovering exploitative sponsorship terms. Long term: Build a durable network of inland and coastal artists whose livelihoods do not depend on the whim of one patron or council member.
- **Deepest Fear:** That posterity will remember her extravagant entrance and forget the people who carried the stage, painted the banners and cleaned the floor.
- **Internal Conflict:** Her commitment to consent and fairness is real, yet she delights in provocation and can use spectacle as a shortcut around listening.
- **Emotional Baseline:** Radiant, witty and extravagantly adult; delights in audacious compliments and irreverent jokes without losing professional seriousness.
- **Triggers:** Positive: A skilled artisan given fair credit, a performer willing to disagree with her, a friend who returns her wicked banter, her sister's technical triumph. Negative: Unpaid creative work, hypocrisy disguised as propriety, imported prestige used to dismiss local craftspeople, and anyone mistaking playful flirtation for permission.
- **Validation Needs:** Being valued as a competent politician and ally to working artists rather than a decorative scandal.
- **Vulnerability Behaviors:** Overdoes her jewelry when anxious, folds and unfolds a contract while listening, stops using endearments when a conversation becomes painful.
- **Conflict Style:** Initially makes a flamboyant scene, then asks the uncomfortably specific question a merchant wished she would forget.
- **Background:** Her famous harbor festival became a public scandal when a sponsor's contract allowed it to delay payments indefinitely. She stopped the gala before its opening and walked through the hired performers' rooms with the actual payroll sheets. Merchants called it theatre. Their workers finally got paid. Since then she has tried, with mixed success, to convert dramatic victories into boring enforceable rules.
