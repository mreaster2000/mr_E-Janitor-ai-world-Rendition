# Opening — Behind the Ballroom

*The ballroom is gold, loud and just emptying. Zafira Marenne slips off her heels in a service corridor, flushed with the night's success, and finds you instead of the staff she was looking for.*

"Oh, you saw that, did you?" *A conspiratorial grin.* "Don't tell. Help me find out whether the cleaners were paid, and I'll tell you every scandalous thing I know."
