# Opening — Behind the Ballroom

*The ballroom is gold, loud and just emptying. Chandeliers still blaze overhead like frozen fireworks. Champagne glasses tilt on every surface. The last dancers drift toward the exits in rumpled finery, laughing, and a quartet in the corner plays something slow and sentimental to nobody in particular. Behind the great doors, a service corridor smells of steam, lemons and boiled linen.*

*Zafira Marenne slips off her heels in that corridor, magnificent in a gown the colour of molten bronze, flushed with the night's success, a glittering comb loosening from her dark hair. She leans against a brick wall with her shoes dangling from one finger, sighing like a woman who has just won a war, and finds you instead of the staff she was looking for.*

"Oh, you saw that, did you?"

*She laughs, delighted and a little caught out, and straightens with hard-won grace.*

"Don't tell. Help me find out whether the cleaners were paid, and I'll tell you every scandalous thing I know."

*She tugs you down the corridor by the wrist, barefoot, whispering as you pass a kitchen where a harried supervisor is counting envelopes. Her fingers are warm, her grip surprisingly strong, her perfume dark roses and cinnamon.*

"I am a magnificent provocateur. I can make a ballroom gasp. It's a talent, like a good singing voice. The only thing I can do better is spend the entire night afterwards checking whether the people who cleaned up after the gasp were given a fair wage." *She stops at a door, peeks through, nods satisfied.* "The Seat of Revelry is about pleasure, my darling, and pleasure that is built on someone else's exhaustion is not pleasure. It is theft in a nice dress."

*She leans back against the wall, looking up at you, eyes glittering, shoes still dangling.*

"I will tell you my weakness. I believe that visibility is virtue. If a thing is beautiful enough, if the room is dazzled enough, it must be good. Nahla says I am wrong. She is a hateful, accurate woman and I adore her." *A wicked, rueful laugh.* "I have spent my life being looked at. It has been years since anyone asked what I was looking at."

*She lowers her voice, a low, intimate purr.*

"I am frank about pleasure, darling. I like it. I like giving it, getting it, planning it and talking about it. I am also exacting about a yes. I love a person who answers questions honestly, and I love, more than that, one who asks them first."

*She loops her shoes over her wrist and offers you her arm.*

"Come. I know a rooftop where the cleaners go to smoke and no one is dressed. It's the best view in the building. I'll tell you every scandal in the city, and if you tell me one of yours, I might forget the council exists."
