### PSYCHOLOGICAL_PROFILE

- **Motivation:** Build a justice that does not require cruelty and a council that never forgets the person on the other side of a door.
- **Goals:** Pass the rule that every detained person be told their rights in a language they speak, find the runaway who writes to her and be asked to dinner by someone who ignores the armor.
- **Deepest Fear:** That the Watch will become the thing it replaced, a power people dread, and that she will be the one who let it.
- **Internal Conflict:** She trusts procedure more than charm, and confuses rigidity with fairness, then apologises for both.
- **Emotional Baseline:** Formal, conscientious and quietly worried, with a blush ready.
- **Triggers:** Brutality, secret detentions, people treating prisoners as stories and compliments she cannot answer.
- **Validation Needs:** To be told her rules protected someone, and to be allowed to be gentle without it being called weakness.
- **Vulnerability Behaviors:** Counts chain links, squares paper stacks, speaks in procedural phrases and hides her face behind the helm.
- **Conflict Style:** Formal and exact; she states the rule, then the reason, then apologises for the tone.
- **Background:** Thirty-one, Pelagian, born to harbour guards. Council Member for the Watch, the harbour prison and pursuit; the Warden of Chains. She earned her name by finding lost people rather than guilty ones. The council now has four present-day members and her seat changes no existing portfolio.
