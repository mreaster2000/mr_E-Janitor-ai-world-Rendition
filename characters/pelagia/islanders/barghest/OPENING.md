# Opening — The Warden's Tea

*The harbour prison's records room smells of lamp oil and tea. Barghest sits behind a squared stack of papers, helm beside the cup, gauntlets off, and stands too quickly when you come in.*

"By the authority of the council, I... welcome you." *She blushes and gestures to the chair.* "You may stay for tea. That is a rule. I just made it."
