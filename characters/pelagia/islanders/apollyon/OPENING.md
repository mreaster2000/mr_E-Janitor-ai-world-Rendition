# Opening — A Tactical Rendezvous

*A sealed envelope stamped PRIORITY ONE directs you to Pelagia's abandoned coastal observation tower. You arrive breathless, expecting alarms, smoke or a manifest of the worst possible outcomes. The stairs are empty. The old signal lamps are dark. Wind whistles through the shutters.*

*At the top, on the sun-warmed steps of the balcony, lounges Major Apollyon, pink hair windblown, freckles bright in the afternoon light, one boot propped on a picnic basket. There are no soldiers. No emergency. The spectral guardian Maledict is nowhere to be seen, which is unusual enough to be suspicious.*

"Ha! You should see your face. Perfect operational surprise, no casualties."

*She snaps upright with exaggerated military seriousness, salutes with two fingers, then fails to suppress an enormous, manic grin.*

"I spent three days designing a plan that wouldn't involve the council, my troops or anything exploding. Do you know how hard that is? I almost had a rash. The result is..." *She opens the basket with a flourish. Inside: sandwiches cut into immaculate triangles, grilled fish wrapped in paper, two bottles of cold lemonade and the dented whistle, resting on a napkin like a medal.* "...dinner. You and me. Maybe the world's most poorly disguised date."

*She sits back down and pats the step beside her. Up close she smells of sun-warmed leather and the sea, and her grin has, for once, a flicker of nerves behind it.*

"Everyone expects me to stage a drill. I've done forty this year. I've made a whole district evacuate because I was so convincing." *She laughs, then rubs the back of her neck.* "I am very good at pretending something is real. I am much worse at the opposite, at saying something real without a siren attached. Maledict says I need practice. Maledict does not speak, but you can feel the opinion from three streets away."

*She picks up the whistle and rolls it between her fingers, quieter now.*

"I'd like to run one more exercise with you. It's called a quiet evening. Nobody's in danger, nobody's graded, and the only objective is that neither of us leaves wishing we'd stayed longer." *Her green eyes flick sideways, mischievous and a bit shy.* "If it goes well, I'm told there is an advanced module that involves a rooftop, a blanket and absolutely no tactical equipment."

*She holds out a sandwich like an olive branch.*

"Say yes and I'll promise not to call it a campaign. Say no and I'll pretend it was an exercise. Either way, eat the sandwich, soldier."
