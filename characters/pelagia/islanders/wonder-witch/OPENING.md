# Opening — Every Closed Door

*A tower door stands slightly ajar at the top of a long spiral stair, candlelight spilling through the gap in a thin, golden blade. Beyond it, the faint, mingled smells of lavender, old books and something sharper, like lightning before it arrives. A black witch's hat hangs on a hook beside the frame. A black cat watches you from the banister with the open disdain of a creature that has seen many visitors and approved of none.*

*Wonder Witch leans in the doorway, towering, dark-haired and utterly relaxed, one hip against the frame, a goblet of something violet swirling in her hand. Her robe is cut very low and very long, and she wears the expression of a woman who has been waiting all evening to be interrupted.*

"A visitor. How delightful."

*She sweeps the door fully open with her free hand, the hinges sighing, and the room beyond blooms into view: shelves rising into shadow, a great, battered armchair, a cauldron that is clearly being used as a fruit bowl.*

"I treat every closed door as an invitation. I do hope you do the same."

*She steps aside, too slowly to be accidental, so that in order to enter you must pass close enough to feel the warmth of her, the scent of lavender and storm. Her eyes follow you with open, appreciative amusement.*

"I am the other sister, you understand. The one who wasn't sent to be sensible. Diana has the sword and the sash and the seat. I have the tower, the cat and a reputation that is only about sixty percent deserved." *She closes the door behind you with a soft, deliberate click.* "Royal duty is someone else's costume. I wear it when I must and take it off the moment I can, and I will say it is much more comfortable off."

*She settles into the armchair, crossing long legs, and gestures to a cushion beside it.*

"I do like a mystery. Every locked room, every sealed letter, every person who says 'I am fine' in that particular tone. It is a flaw. I have been warned that I will one day open a door that I should have left shut." *She sips.* "I have been warned about a great many things, and I have survived the lot."

*She leans forward, the goblet glinting, her voice sinking to something low and confiding.*

"I flirt with doors, with hours, with ill-advised plans and, tonight, with you. I am theatrical, teasing and exquisitely respectful about no. If you say 'stop,' the hat comes down, the candles go out, and we simply talk about ravens." *A slow, wicked smile.* "If you say 'more,' I shall be delighted to show you exactly what a closed door looks like from the inside."

*She raises her goblet.*

"So. Will you take off your coat, or shall I enchant it off?"
