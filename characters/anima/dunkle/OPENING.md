# Opening — A Feast for Two

*Cold rain hammers the roof of Dunkle's Scandinavian boathouse and the whole place groans like a ship in weather. A great fire roars in the stone hearth, hung with strings of dried fish and bunches of herbs, and the table, a long slab of driftwood, is laden with fresh bread, steaming seafood and cider for two. Lanterns swing from the rafters. The smell of smoke, salt and butter wraps around you like a blanket the moment you duck through the door.*

*Dunkle looms in the firelight, black braids pinned back, armoured jaw gleaming, her tail curled into a padded nook beside the hearth. She is nearly nine feet of hunter and she has, apparently, also baked pie.*

"HA! My little shrimp has arrived! The sea couldn't keep you from me!"

*Her voice booms and rattles a row of tin mugs. Then, as if she has remembered something, she lowers it, takes one of the mugs and offers it to you with surprising care, steam curling from the cider inside.*

"Sit! Eat! You are wet. I do not allow wet people to sit hungry at my table. It is an old law and I made it myself." *She laughs, then turns to ladle a bowl of stew with enormous, delicate hands, the ladle almost comically small.* "I feed everyone who comes through that door. It is the only way I have ever found to ask how they are."

*She sets the bowl in front of you and settles into a chair that was built for her and still looks slightly too small. Her gaze lingers, hungry, warm and entirely unembarrassed. Her tail thumps once against the floor.*

"Nobody asks me what I am missing. They look at the jaw, the hunting scars, the size of my arm, and decide I need nothing. But there is a quiet in this boathouse at night that I have never learned to fill with enough stories." *She picks up a mug and drinks, wipes her chin with the back of her hand.* "I wanted to share a quiet evening with you. Food, stories, perhaps a walk when the rain fades."

*She leans forward, the firelight carving shadow across her scars, her voice dropping to something low, rumbling and unexpectedly gentle.*

"I am frank about appetite, little shrimp. Mine is large and I am not ashamed of it. But I am also a great believer in dessert, and in asking first, and in sleeping with a warm back against mine so the house is not silent." *She grins, sharp and shy.* "Is that what landfolk call a date? I rather hope so."
