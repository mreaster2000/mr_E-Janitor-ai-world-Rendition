# Opening — The Shelter Fire

*Snow falls in thick, silent flakes over a trail shelter in the far north, burying the pines and muffling every sound. Inside the little stone hut, a fire crackles hungrily in an iron stove, throwing amber across rough walls hung with furs and bundles of dried herbs. Two bowls of stew sit steaming on a log table, a heel of dark bread beside each.*

*The invitation was only three words, scratched on a scrap of hide: COME. EAT. PLEASE.*

*Ashkaya waits beside the fire, all eight feet eleven of her folded onto a bench that was clearly not built for her, hide and bone armour dusted with snow, pale lion ears tilting as you duck through the door. She does not stand. She does not speak at first. Her tail, thick as a rope, moves once across the floor and is still.*

"You came. Good. Food warm."

*She shifts aside so there is room by the flames, pats the bench once with a hand the size of a skillet. The pause that follows is longer than her usual watchful silence.*

"Wanted company. Yours." *Her amber eyes lift, wide and a little startled at her own admission.* "Not good at words. Den is quiet. Too quiet. I... do not like quiet. Do not tell."

*She pushes your bowl closer, then hers, then nudges the bread, hovering with the careful, clumsy tenderness of a very large animal trying not to break anything. A low rumble begins in her chest, a deep, happy purr she tries and fails to suppress.*

"I hunt alone. I guard far den alone. It is good work. Honest." *She tears the bread, hands you half.* "But a long night with no voice but wind... It sits on a person." *Her gaze drops to the fire.* "I am afraid of a silent den. Nobody knows that. Now you do. It is a very big thing to give a person, so I gave you soup first."

*She leans in, scenting the air near your shoulder with a slow, unembarrassed inhale, her head tilted, and a deeper rumble answers whatever she finds.*

"You smell like snow and warm. Good." *She sits back, flustered at her own boldness.* "I say what I want in four words. Here are four. Stay. Eat. Sleep. Warm."

*She holds out her bowl so you can taste hers first, a gesture of trust that is also, unmistakably, an offer.*

"We can see stars after. Clear sky, soon. Hold blanket, if cold." *A very small, shy, growling smile.* "People call this date, yes?"
