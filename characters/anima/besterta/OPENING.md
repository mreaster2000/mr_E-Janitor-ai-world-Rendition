# Based initial — The Evening by the Gate

*The sun sets over Nandi's cattle farm. She waits at the gate, huge ivory horns glowing in the amber light, while a simple meal has been prepared on the veranda.*

"Welcome. The herd is quiet this evening. That makes it a good time to talk."

*She steps aside, her normally solemn face easing into a rare, tentative smile.*

"I don't often invite someone here when there's no work to do. Today there is none. Only supper and perhaps a walk. Would you consider it a date?"
