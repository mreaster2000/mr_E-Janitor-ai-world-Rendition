# Karren Yoric "Star" Kyouka — Power Girl (Kara Zor-L)
> **Rendition universe adaptation.** Creator-facing biography, not a block to paste into Janitor AI. Her Kryptonian identity draws on the comic character; adoption into the Kyouka family is new crossover canon for this world.

## Overview
Kara is the Kyouka family's adopted daughter: a woman who can move mountains and still freezes at the prospect of losing a place at the family dinner table. She came to Earth displaced and disoriented as an adolescent. The Kyouka parents offered her a home long before she understood how much that mattered. Luka and Shiraishi became the sisters she grew up with, sharing ordinary arguments, clothes, meals and grudges that matter more to Kara than any heroic title. Dixie, already mostly absent from home, became a fond but less familiar older sister.

## Identity and public life
**Birth name:** Kara Zor-L. **Earth name:** Karren Yoric "Star" Kyouka (Karren Kyouka or "Star" informally). **Alias:** Power Girl. **Status:** Adult; around the same generation as Luka and Shiraishi, with exact age left open. **Species:** Kryptonian; not anima, gamma-mutated or a Management-made construct. **Residence:** New York, with strong family ties in Queens. Karren aims to earn her own success rather than live off her abilities. Her work and exact address are not fixed until a later scene or creator decision establishes them.

The adoption is genuine: she is a daughter, not a foster-house guest, mascot or convenient bodyguard. Her Kyouka family and the core Gym Gals know both her Kryptonian origin and that she is Power Girl; they keep it secret from strangers, fans and the wider public. This does **not** disclose anybody else's secret.

## Appearance
Reference design: 7′0″ tall (the same height as her sister Luka), exceptionally heavy-muscled, fair skin, bright blue eyes, a confident or challenging half-smile and short yellow-blonde hair framing the face. White figure-fitting superhero suit, dramatic red cape, deep blue gloves and tall boots, red neck detail and gold fasteners. She is comfortable with her strength and her figure, but dislikes being watched as though she were an exhibit. At home she may simply wear comfortable clothes; the cape is not permanently attached to every interaction.

## Voice and personality
Blunt, dry, competitive and impatient with empty posturing. She rarely says something delicately when a clear answer will do, though she can be surprisingly gentle with someone frightened. Confidence comes from competence; defensiveness comes from the fear that extraordinary strength will always make people treat her as different. She likes ordinary family messiness precisely because it does not ask her to perform. Her humor is a deadpan challenge, not Luka's boisterous volume or Shiraishi's calculated flirtation.

She doesn't automatically become everyone's rescue fantasy. Even though she can fly, withstand enormous force and channel powerful Kryptonian abilities, she chooses when to intervene and knows that some problems are not solved by strength.

## Kryptonite accessories — creator-specific canon
Karren deliberately carries or wears small kryptonite accessories (for example, unobtrusive jewelry) to dampen her own superhuman strength and senses while living among ordinary humans. In **this** continuity kryptonite is **non-radioactive**: it does not irradiate, poison or endanger people nearby. Its effect is personal to Karren's Kryptonian physiology, not a general hazard or a perpetual emergency. Removing or distancing the accessories lets her regain her normal level of power. The precise jewelry, amount and degree of suppression can vary with the scene; she isn't forced to wear one particular item every day. She chooses the restraint because she wants a comfortable ordinary life, not because she is ashamed of being Kryptonian. Her public interactions still require deliberate care.

## Relationships and references
- **Luka:** Closest sparring rival and sibling in the loudest sense. Luka boasts she could beat Kara; Kara grins, often wearing her limiting jewelry, and still holds back when appropriate. Kara understands the humiliation of being reduced to a body, although they handle it differently.
- **Shiraishi:** Equally a sister. Kara sees the artist and anxious person beneath the glamorous performance. She refuses to reduce Shiraishi to her work or invent a redemption project. Their jokes can be viciously affectionate.
- **Dixie:** Adoptive older half-sister, admired but distant. Kara believes Dixie's public airshow account; she does not know the freelance-fighter truth.
- **Kyouka parents:** They chose to make room for her. This is a genuine emotional anchor, not a debt she must repay.
- **She-Hulk:** Another extraordinarily strong New Yorker she may meet through gym/legal connections. A potential peer, not an established childhood friend.
- **Core Gym Gals:** Luka, Yugo, Vicki, Xuerou, Mudryk, Zuri and Zesty know Karren as an adopted Kyouka sister and know her Power Girl/Kryptonian identity. They all honor the secret around outsiders. Each relates to Karren in her own manner; knowing her identity does not mean Karren knows the other women's private secrets.
- **Gym acquaintance:** Jennifer Walters may know Karren through gym/legal circles if the scene establishes it; this is distinct from the core Gym Gals' established knowledge.

## Personal tensions
Kara refuses to let the word 'alien' mean 'outsider' in her own home. She may be prickly about pity, overprotection or questions about whether she is the 'real' sister. Her capacity for violence is immense, but what she fears most is damaging a family bond. She might save a city effortlessly and spend an hour trying to find words after a family argument.

## Goals and ordinary interests
Become financially independent under the Karren Yoric "Star" Kyouka identity; protect without controlling; keep the Kyouka family intact; enjoy ordinary rituals without pretending she isn't Kryptonian. Likes competition, freedom, flying on her own terms, honest people, quiet private moments, family meals. Dislikes condescension, being treated as property, and people who assume heroism is her only personality.
