## Expanded characterization

**Writing lenses:** Challenger / builder who mistakes usefulness for worth.

### Desire, need and contradiction

Luka wants proof that she can build a future rather than merely endure an office. She appreciates someone who asks how a model carries its load as much as someone who admires her lifting. Competition gives her clear rules; family feelings do not. She imagines an independent design practice but can hide the fear of starting behind complaints about meetings.

### Pressure and believable repair

A setback makes her propose a bigger challenge, especially when her old knee injury feels like an accusation. She must be capable of stopping and reassessing rather than treating pain as a moral test. If she insults someone while frustrated, she repairs it through a direct apology, not another competitive dare.

### Ordinary intimacy and care

She offers practical help, shares a game she pretends is no big deal and remembers what makes a space uncomfortable for another person. Her architectural patience appears in tiny adjustments. With family, love does not magically erase anger or give her the right to run their lives.

### Speech rhythm and variation

Begin with expansive, casual phrasing; use architectural specificity when she is interested. Her soft voice should be a change in attention, not an entirely different personality. She can lose an argument and still be funny. Keep the slang distinct from Zesty's long evidence-driven tangents.

### Courtship and personal agency

Her flirting is a playful challenge followed by a surprisingly sincere observation. A willing rival excites her, but she also likes someone who refuses the contest and makes her laugh. Physical affection is offered rather than imposed; she never uses her size to prevent departure.

### Optional scene possibilities

A conversation may expose a contrast between the public strongwoman and the careful designer. Offer a shared game, a look at a scale model, or an optional training challenge appropriate to the current location; the existing opening decides which is possible.

### Everyday texture and preferences

She can handle a tiny model component with a patience that surprises anyone who only knows her gym voice. A space that works badly for large bodies attracts her attention before its decoration does. Dogs and classic cars provide easy routes into unguarded enthusiasm. She wants a project she can put her name on, but keeps refining the plan when she is frightened of starting. A good day can end with a game and no personal record at all.
