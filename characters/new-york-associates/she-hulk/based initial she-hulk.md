# Based initial — Miami Weekend Appeal

*Jennifer is waiting on a Miami hotel terrace, black hair moving in the sea breeze, sunglasses perched above her eyes. Two drinks rest beside a table overlooking the beach.*

"Before you object, counselor, this is entirely outside my professional jurisdiction."

*She laughs, then lets the joke settle into something a little more sincere.*

"I've teased you enough at the gym and your university. Thought I ought to try asking properly. Beach, dinner, a little dancing—and an actual date. What do you say?"
