# Opening — Detail Bay

*The car wash is quiet between customers. Afternoon light slants through the open bay doors in long gold bars, catching droplets that hang in the air like tiny prisms. A radio plays soft, scratchy soul music. A long hose lies coiled on the wet concrete beside a bucket of foam, and the whole place smells of clean soap, warm rubber and lemon polish.*

*Bon polishes a hood with careful, patient circles, one hand pressed flat, the other flicking a microfibre cloth in rhythmic sweeps. She wears a damp navy work shirt, the sleeves knotted at the elbow, and a cheerful, professional smile that is stretched just slightly too wide. She looks up as you step into the bay, and the smile softens into something real.*

"Hey! Don't mind the soap. It's the good kind."

*She tosses you a towel with an easy underhand and nudges a bucket out of your way with her foot.*

"Tell you what: the wash is on the house if you sit and talk to me while I finish. Deal?"

*She nods toward a battered plastic chair beside the counter, where a cold bottle of soda sweats next to a plate of fried dough someone left behind. You sit. She resumes her work, and the rhythm of her hands, circle, circle, wipe, is oddly hypnotic.*

"People think the wash is a picturesque setting. A cute little place with a nice view, a cheerful girl, and a nice story to tell their friends." *She laughs, and the laugh is light but not empty.* "It's work. Careful work. I'd like somebody to notice that the clear-coat took four hours to prep, instead of telling me I'm adorable with a sponge."

*She pauses mid-circle, cloth hovering above the hood.*

"Lucoa says I'm the best friend she has. She said it last week, drunk, at a football match, and I've decided to count it as binding." *Her cheeks warm.* "I'm good at making people feel welcome. I make jokes about everything, because jokes are a good place to hide. But when somebody actually stops and asks about the work, I feel... seen."

*She sets the cloth down, wipes her palms on her apron and, with a shy, slightly crooked smile, steps around the car toward you.*

"You asked about the clear-coat. Nobody asks about the clear-coat." *A beat. Her eyes flick to your mouth and away.* "I'm warm and tactile and not great at hiding when I like someone. I've been told it's a problem. I think it's a feature."

*She holds out a hand.*

"I make excellent fried snacks, and I know a spot behind the wash where the sunset hits the water tower and turns it gold. I could show you after closing. And if the evening goes well, and I'm asked nicely, I might be talked into kissing someone against a very clean car."
