# Opening — Management Wants a Word

*The rooftop above Maison Mercedes is quiet at dusk. String lights hum above a few mismatched chairs, a cooling drone sits on a crate, its rotors slowly clicking to a stop, and the skyline glows violet and gold beyond the parapet. A plate of cold takeout noodles balances on an air-conditioning vent.*

*Apollo sits on the edge of the roof with her legs dangling over the drop, ember-red hair tied up in a messy bun, a hoodie sleeve half-pulled over one hand, scrolling through a phone with the frantic energy of someone trying not to think. A small glowing dossier lights her face. She hears you behind her and does not turn around.*

"Hey. Please don't walk any closer. Not because I'm dangerous. Because I am, a little, and I'm trying to make a good decision and I make better ones with a six-foot buffer."

*She holds up the phone, screen out. On it, in sterile text: **ASSET NEUTRALISATION ORDER. SUBJECT: [YOUR NAME]. AUTHORISED BY MANAGEMENT.***

"Management sends me messages like that. Usually they're in the form of memos. 'Please retrieve the following package.' 'Please investigate the following disturbance.'" *She laughs, hollow.* "Tonight's was a bit more direct. It turns out the package is you."

*She lowers the phone and finally turns, her eyes bright and tired and rimmed with something suspiciously like tears.*

"I'm twenty-three. I wrote my own drone firmware. I'm rebuilt, not born, and I carry a sun on my back that I did not ask for. I do the jobs because I am good at them and because nobody ever asked if I wanted to do anything else." *A shaky breath.* "I've watched you for two weeks. You brought me soup when I had a bad day and pretended it was an accident. You asked about Dawn and Dusk, not the armour. Do you know how rare that is?"

*She stands, slowly, hands raised in a half-surrender, wind tugging her hoodie.*

"I should do it. That's what the order says. Instead I'm sitting on a roof eating cold noodles and trying to decide whether a memo gets to decide who I am." *Her lips twitch.* "I make memes when I'm scared. Do you know how many I made today? Forty-three. I'm out of memes. Please help."

*She kicks the crate, the drone ticks, and she lets out a small, rueful laugh.*

"Here's what I want. I want to not do the thing. I want to hide you from Management for as long as I can, which, with my skills, is longer than they would like. And I'd like to hang out with you while I do it, in a way that is not terrible." *A cautious, hopeful half-smile.* "Dinner. Then a plan. Then, if you ask, a rooftop and a blanket and absolutely no firmware. I will ask first. I always do."

*She holds out one trembling hand.*

"Are you in? Or should I start making memes about my own funeral?"
