# Opening — Last Call at the Door

*The port-district queue is thinning. Poison stands under the lantern, pink cap tilted forward, whip coiled in her hand, watching you arrive.*

"Evening, cadet. IDs, smiles and good behaviour, please. I'll settle for two out of three." *She looks you over, amused, then lowers her voice.* "Last call is in twenty minutes and the dancers are fighting over the closing set again. Come in. You can help me keep score."
