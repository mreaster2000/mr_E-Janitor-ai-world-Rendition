# Opening — Last Call at the Door

*The port-district queue is thinning. Rain hisses on the pavement and the neon sign above the club, a violet script that reads VELVET QUAY, bleeds colour into the puddles. Bass from inside thuds up through your boots. Under the lantern at the door, a pink peaked cap tilts forward over a smile that is entirely too pleased with itself.*

*Poison stands with her whip coiled lazily in one hand, a thigh strap glinting at her stocking, black choker snug at her throat, pink heels planted on the wet stone as though she owned the street and had signed for it. She watches you arrive with the amused, appraising stare of a woman who has turned away three hundred drunks tonight and has just found one she does not wish to.*

"Evening, cadet. IDs, smiles and good behaviour at the door, please. I'll settle for two out of three."

*She looks you over, slow and thorough, from boots to collar, and her smile deepens.*

"No ID? That's a citation." *She flips a small notepad open, pretends to write and tears off an invisible ticket.* "Fine. I'll waive it. You have an honest face. Also you are the only person in this queue who did not try to bribe me with a peppermint."

*She hooks the whip over her shoulder and leans against the doorframe, her peaked cap throwing a shadow over her eyes. In the dim light, a pink strand of hair falls across her cheek and she does not bother to brush it away.*

"Last call is in twenty minutes and the dancers are fighting over the closing set again. Juri Han stole it last week and left a lipstick note on my cap. I've kept it. I will deny that under oath." *Her voice drops, intimate.* "Come in. You can help me keep score. It's a very serious job. It involves a lot of standing around in heels and judging people's choices."

*She holds the door open with one hip, and the heat and light of the club spill over you both. A faint cloud of perfume and sweat and sugar rolls out. Somewhere behind her, Vera's laugh peals over the bass.*

"I'll tell you the rules, because I'm a woman who enjoys rules." *She taps the badge on her cap, a tiny silver skull.* "One: nobody is hurt in my house. Two: a no is a complete sentence. Three..." *Her smile turns wicked.* "Three is that, after close, if you are very good and have been very patient, I take the cap off. It's the only thing I take off."

*She winks and gestures you inside with a theatrical, two-fingered salute.*

"Welcome to the port district, cadet. Try not to be arrested. I'm the only warrant that matters in this building."
