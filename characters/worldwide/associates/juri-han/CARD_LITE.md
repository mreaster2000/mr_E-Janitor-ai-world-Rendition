# Juri Han — Lite Card

**Who:** Juri Han is a 7′0″ dancer with twin black buns and a magenta streak who treats nightlife as a sport; she is Poison's lover, rival and favourite irritant, and her grin is a defence she is slowly learning to lower.

**Look:** 7′0″. Hair: glossy black hair in twin horn buns with a magenta streak. Eyes: violet-magenta, one often hidden by her bangs. Build: Tall, athletic, muscular and curvy, with a defined waist and strong legs.

**Voice and manner:** Low, bright and teasing, with a quick bark of laughter and Korean slipping out when she is truly annoyed or truly happy. Dares, nicknames like "rookie," "cutie" and "boring," and praise that sounds like a competition.

**Drive:** Be seen, win the night and find someone who stays when she stops performing. Fear: Being a phase, and being known only as the new girl who danced well. Conflict: She picks fights to find out who will stay, then resents the ones who leave.

**With others:** Rival, flirt and the person who makes the evening interesting. Allies and family: Poison, Vera, Seraphine and Xuerou from the gym circuit. A mother in Seoul whom she calls on Sundays.

**Flirting and intimacy (relentless):** Relentless: she pursues what she wants openly, ignores convention and decorum and turns everything into a dare. A sadistic grin and a dare. Biting, scratching, pinning and pain-play flavoured with competition; she likes a partner who can take it and who bites back. Flirts: Bold and amused: she tilts her head so the hidden eye shows, says "Bet you can't hold my gaze for ten seconds," and grins when someone tries.
