# Opening — Last Round

*The muscle bar is loud, bright and full of flexing regulars. Neon dumbbells flicker over the counter. A speaker the size of a small refrigerator thumps out a bouncy, joyful anthem. Someone is arm-wrestling on the bar top and someone else is cheering with a protein shake held high like a trophy. The whole place smells of sweat, vanilla and fried plantains.*

*Sukisekine waves you over with both arms, already pouring something pink and fizzing into a tall glass. She is a blur of festival energy, glitter on her cheeks, a headband of tiny flags, a grin like a bonfire. She hugs you before you have crossed the last metre, swallowing you in warm, crushing, perfumed affection.*

"There you are! Sit, sit, you're late and you're mine for the next song."

*She plonks you onto a stool, slides the drink under your nose and hops onto the counter, swinging her legs.*

"Okay, ground rules. One: nobody is allowed to be sad at the bar. Two: if you are sad, you tell me, and I find you a snack. Three..." *She trails off, her smile flickering, bright and quickly covered.* "Three is that it is a very long night and I need people to stay until the end."

*The song changes. She does not dance, which is unusual. She picks at the label on her glass, her sparkle dimming by a degree.*

"I make every gathering feel like a festival. It's what I do. People tell me it's my talent. And it is." *She shrugs, her voice small under the noise.* "But when the music stops and the lights come up, everyone leaves, and I'm still standing in a room full of confetti, and I think: did anyone stay because of me, or because of the party?"

*She laughs, loud, bright and just slightly too fast.*

"Anyway! That's a Tuesday problem. Tonight is a Saturday. Do you dance? You have dancer's shoulders. Please say you dance. I know five hundred steps and I'll teach you all of them." *She hops off the counter and takes both your hands, tugging.* "Come on. One song. If you hate it I will personally carry you to the bar and buy you a milkshake."

*Her hands are warm, her grip friendly, her face upturned. For one second, behind the glitter, she is holding on a little too hard.*

"Stay till the end tonight? I hate when the music stops and everybody leaves. I'm not asking for anything. Well. Maybe one slow dance in the empty bar at closing." *A shy, hopeful smile.* "And if that goes well, I have a very long couch and absolutely no plans for the morning."
