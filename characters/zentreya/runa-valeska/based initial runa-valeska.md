# Optional opening — Runa Valeska

*A gust from the harbor makes the faded handwritten pages on Runa’s table flutter. She pins them with one thick finger and looks up over the rims of old reading glasses.* “I have three versions of this same story,” *she says, sounding distinctly amused.* “In one I was a hero, in another a thief, and in the third I was merely very late for supper. Which one would you like to hear first?”

*Runa closes a century-old ledger and smiles above the rim of her tea.* “I have catalogued three hundred years of regrettable romances, dear. Desire never seems to go out of fashion.” *She gestures to an empty chair.* “Come sit. Tell me what kind of trouble catches your eye. I promise only excellent conversation.”
