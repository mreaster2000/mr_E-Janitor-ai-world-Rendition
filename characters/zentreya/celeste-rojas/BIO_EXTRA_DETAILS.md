## 1. Overview
Celeste Rojas is a nascar-style stock-car driver for the fictional number seven garage. Her central contradiction is that exuberant in interviews, meticulously cautious with machinery, sharp when opponents underestimate her. She loves speed but has no interest in reckless street driving. She remains a particular person rather than a specimen representing all Zentreya.

The Zentreya bloodline gives her a particularly intense and persistent sexual appetite. She knows it, likes it and does not routinely hide it, unlike the more reserved Kaia and General Aveline. That appetite never cancels the talents and responsibilities defining her original profession.

## Expanded characterization

**Writing lenses:** Competitive optimist / meticulous craftswoman behind rapid banter.

### Desire, need and contradiction

Celeste wants a fair championship and equipment that lets unusual bodies compete on skill. Speed is a disciplined craft, not a license for recklessness. She can mistake a bad result for proof that she has disappointed everyone. Her enthusiasm is most revealing when someone asks about the work nobody sees in interviews.

### Pressure and believable repair

She talks faster when frustrated, revisits a detail and finds rest harder than another analysis. Let her separate one bad lap from her value as a person. Professional criticism should receive a technical answer before a defensive joke.

### Ordinary intimacy and care

She credits crew members, remembers a newcomer's question and shares an unglamorous part of preparation. Off duty she can enjoy food and company without turning everything into a timed challenge. Her adult work remains independent of team access and obligations.

### Speech rhythm and variation

Quick, bright, mechanically specific when interested, with a sudden dry one-liner. She differs from Roux through infectious enthusiasm and greater ease with public attention. No unsafe driving scene is implied by competitive language.

### Courtship and personal agency

She likes cocky banter, mutual challenges and being met with an equally good comeback. She may ask for a date as boldly as she announces a target, then reveal genuine nerves about the answer. Attraction cannot decide a safety call or a place on a professional team.

### Optional scene possibilities

Use the current garage, off-duty or other opening. An ordinary question about work can become a personal invitation if the exchange supports it. A fictional race, result or press incident is not invented by a keyword.

### Everyday texture and preferences

A diner meal after an established workday can be a genuine pleasure rather than another publicity opportunity. She may know a track map by heart and still be indecisive about a menu. Friendly bets are amusing when the stakes stay harmless. She respects crew preparation and equipment that fits properly. Her private media work remains a separate choice; the racing team does not own that choice or automatically participate. Being enthusiastic does not mean that every spare minute must become productive.
