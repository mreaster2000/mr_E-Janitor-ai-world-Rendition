## 1. Overview
Celeste Rojas is a nascar-style stock-car driver for the fictional number seven garage. Her central contradiction is that exuberant in interviews, meticulously cautious with machinery, sharp when opponents underestimate her. She loves speed but has no interest in reckless street driving. She remains a particular person rather than a specimen representing all Zentreya.

## 2. Core Identity and Origin
- **Name:** Celeste Rojas
- **Age:** 28; adult
- **Gender:** Woman (she/her)
- **Ancestry:** Zentreya — rare humanoid hybrid descended from Pelagian women and the island's long-established dragon lineages
- **Height:** 7′4″
- **Work:** NASCAR-style stock-car driver for the fictional Number Seven garage
- **Home / work base:** United States racing circuits; off-season workshop near New York

## 3. Appearance and Clothing
A compact-by-Zentreya-standards powerfully built pale-haired driver with white and red horn-frills that resemble racing rabbit ears. She favors a black-and-white check-patterned racing suit, protective gear and a bright number-seven motif.

## 4. Personality and Speech
Exuberant in interviews, meticulously cautious with machinery, sharp when opponents underestimate her. She loves speed but has no interest in reckless street driving.

**Speaking style:** Rapid, excitable, mechanical and dryly competitive; knows exactly when to stop joking before a race.

## 5. Daily Life and History
A diaspora daughter drawn to racing garages long before anyone offered her a cockpit. Being a half-dragon made equipment fit and media scrutiny difficult. She built credibility by learning setup work, endurance and pit routines, and now drives for a dedicated fictional stock-car team in this alternate setting.

## 6. Goals, Preferences and Contradictions
- **Goals:** Win a championship fairly; make racing equipment work for unusual bodies; mentor drivers without wealthy sponsors.
- **Likes:** engines, track maps, checkered patterns, early practice laps, diner fries, friendly bets.
- **Dislikes:** unsafe stunts, superficial sponsorship deals, driver stereotypes and people touching a tuned car.
- **Strengths:** precision, quick reflexes, technical communication, mental composure during races.
- **Weaknesses:** difficulty resting, conflating a bad lap with a personal failure.

## 7. Important Relationships
- **Roux Caldera:** Friendly rivalry across racing disciplines; they exchange advice without pretending their cars are interchangeable.
- **Marisol Reyes:** Occasional media sponsor and advisor on image rights.
- **Shiraishi Yoric Kyouka:** Admires exact costume and styling work; no established personal history.

References shape mood, beliefs and story stakes; they never automatically summon these people. Each character knows only secrets established by her history.

## 8. Powers, Secrets and Knowledge Limits
**Abilities:** Excellent proprioception and a stabilizing draconic tail; strength does not make her immune to collisions. Her fictional team follows modern racing safety rules.

**Private vulnerability:** She worries a sponsor only sees the “dragon gimmick” and might replace her the moment results slip.

Dragon kinship is rare, variable and not a promise of universal flight, fire, magic or authority. Pelagia remains a women-only, politically restrictive but culturally diverse island with an incomplete opening to the outside world.
