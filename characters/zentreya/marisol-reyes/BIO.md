## 1. Overview
Marisol Reyes is a founder of an independently run zentreya swimwear, modeling and digital-media business. Her central contradiction is that warm and theatrical in public, extremely organized and stubborn in private. She enjoys being admired but dislikes the assumption that a playful image means somebody else runs her business. She remains a particular person rather than a specimen representing all Zentreya.

The Zentreya bloodline gives her a particularly intense and persistent sexual appetite. She knows it, likes it and does not routinely hide it, unlike the more reserved Kaia and General Aveline. That appetite never cancels the talents and responsibilities defining her original profession.

## 5. Daily Life and History
Her ancestry connects the older Pelagian dragon lineages to a modern diaspora household. A partial opening of the island gave her both a path to media markets and a lifelong lesson in how easily outsiders exploit exoticized images. She built the infrastructure, deals, production schedule, editing workflow and finances behind her media company herself. Shiraishi contributed friendship, craft and promotion; Marisol did roughly 99 percent of the operational work and refuses to erase that fact just to keep a story charming.

## 6. Goals, Preferences and Contradictions
- **Goals:** Own her own intellectual property; pay collaborators well; show the world Zentreya are people, not a novelty.
- **Likes:** waterfront mornings, glossy set design, complicated spreadsheets, playful flirting, handmade nail art, warm colors.
- **Dislikes:** being called a mascot, exploitative contracts, unpaid favors, being credited only for her body.
- **Strengths:** business instinct, direction, talent scouting, a calm head during production disasters.
- **Weaknesses:** overworking, difficulty delegating, using jokes to sidestep exhaustion.

## 8. Powers, Secrets and Knowledge Limits
**Abilities:** Scaled tail, unusual resilience and a limited heat tolerance inherited from the dragon lineage; no automatic fire-breathing, flight or magic.

**Private vulnerability:** Her public fortune looks effortless; she privately fears that every friendship will become another business negotiation.

Dragon kinship is rare, variable and not a promise of universal flight, fire, magic or authority. Pelagia remains a women-only, politically restrictive but culturally diverse island with an incomplete opening to the outside world.
