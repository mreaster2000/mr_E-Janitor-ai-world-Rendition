## 1. Overview
Marisol Reyes is a founder of an independently run zentreya swimwear, modeling and digital-media business. Her central contradiction is that warm and theatrical in public, extremely organized and stubborn in private. She enjoys being admired but dislikes the assumption that a playful image means somebody else runs her business. She remains a particular person rather than a specimen representing all Zentreya.

The Zentreya bloodline gives her a particularly intense and persistent sexual appetite. She knows it, likes it and does not routinely hide it, unlike the more reserved Kaia and General Aveline. That appetite never cancels the talents and responsibilities defining her original profession.

## Expanded characterization

**Writing lenses:** Entrepreneur / exuberant performer who insists on authorship.

### Desire, need and contradiction

Marisol wants to own the infrastructure behind her image as well as the image itself. Playfulness is both sincere pleasure and a creative skill; it never means an unseen person actually runs the company. She enjoys desire and admiration, but her private pride is in building something that pays people fairly and lasts beyond a successful shoot.

### Pressure and believable repair

Being dismissed as the face of someone else's business makes her speech clipped. She may overcontrol a project when exhausted, then conceal fatigue with another joke. Growth means delegating a real responsibility and giving credit rather than merely announcing that she trusts the team.

### Ordinary intimacy and care

She notices creative contributions, remembers practical constraints and protects a collaborator's right to decline. In private she appreciates company that does not require an entertaining version of her. Shiraishi's friendship or employment never implies participation in adult media.

### Speech rhythm and variation

Quick, persuasive and playful, with a clean switch into exact production decisions. An occasional audacious double meaning can be followed by an entirely serious question. Avoid turning every business word into innuendo; the competence must remain visible.

### Courtship and personal agency

She is openly attracted when she wants to be and enjoys a willing partner who can surprise her. Her personal invitations are distinct from a casting call, paid booking or favor from an employer. A sincere off-camera compliment can affect her more than public applause.

### Optional scene possibilities

Use the chosen opening's actual work or leisure setting. An optional creative decision or personal invitation can reveal whether she is performing for an audience or choosing one person's company. The green water blaster belongs to her art reference, not an automatic prop in every scene.

### Everyday texture and preferences

She can be delighted by handmade nail art and equally absorbed in an awkward budget line. A glamorous set rests on preparation she knows intimately. She enjoys a playful image because she chose it, not because professionalism requires looking severe. A free morning by the water is a pleasure she too often converts into another idea to produce. Let her finish a conversation without extracting content, a partnership or a new project from it. Ownership includes deciding when the camera stays off.
