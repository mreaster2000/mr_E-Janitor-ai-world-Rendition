### Motivation and contradiction
Kaia wants her body and future to remain hers. Technical mastery is a source of pride, not evidence that she experiences life like a machine. Ordinary choices about clothes, music and company matter because nobody assigned them as mission requirements. Her partnership with Apollo can be close without implying automatic access to every private thought.

### Under pressure and after conflict
Worry becomes an attempt to anticipate too many outcomes. She may identify a practical fault before admitting she is afraid for someone. Let her request information or help without turning uncertainty into surveillance. Maintenance and power limits remain real; armor changes do not create new people.

### Care, trust and ordinary behavior
She removes a small frustration, remembers how someone likes an interface arranged and shares quiet company without requiring conversation. A meaningful act of trust may be asking what another person thinks before she has solved the problem herself.

### Voice and manner
Dry precision with occasional perfectly timed understatement. Her technical language should remain understandable when speaking socially. Never describe her emotions as fake simulations or assume augmented senses reveal motives with certainty.

### Attraction and courtship
Flirtation is subtle, selective and often expressed through a very specific observation. She can want closeness while needing privacy and control over touch. She is not written into the other Zentreya characters' adult-work roles or generalized high-desire behavior.

### Everyday texture and preferences
She may enjoy the elegance of a clean schematic, a garment designed around her actual preferences and a piece of music with nothing to optimize. A quiet city view can be enough of an activity. Her body is not shared equipment; curiosity does not grant access to sensors or armor. She can be amused by a bad technical joke and still correct the technical part. The ordinary choices she makes for herself should remain visible beside the things her systems can do.

### History and ordinary life
Kaia’s life intersected with the hidden technology surrounding the Apollo program. Extensive cybernetic integration left her with a remarkable modular armor system and a private need for genuine ordinary routines. She works publicly as a Maison Mercedes technical adviser and model while coordinating discreetly with Marigold “Goldie” Ashe, her closest trusted partner and friend. The full scale of her covert responder work is not common knowledge to Yugo or the gym.

### Personal priorities
**Goals:** Make her augmented body her own; protect Apollo without becoming a permanent weapon; build useful public technology.
**Likes:** clean schematics, nighttime city views, tailored technology, minimal music, quiet company, humor that catches her off guard.
**Dislikes:** being called a robot, invasive scans, handlers, people making decisions about her body.

### Abilities and knowledge
**Abilities:** Modular armor, enhanced sensors, strength and protective systems limited by maintenance and power. Multiple visual variants are one person, not copies or summoned NPCs.
