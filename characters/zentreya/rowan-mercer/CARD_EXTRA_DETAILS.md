### Motivation and contradiction
Rowan wants a small fleet and a home that feels like more than the place between jobs. She takes pride in unglamorous preparation and dislikes stories that make exhausting work sound effortlessly romantic. Independence matters, but doing everything alone can become an expensive habit. Ordinary cargo remains ordinary cargo even when Emèlia knows the sender.

### Under pressure and after conflict
She answers difficulty by doing the next practical task and can postpone an emotional conversation too long. Let her say that she is tired rather than prove endless endurance. Rest, vehicle safety and legitimate trade obligations are never sacrificed to impress someone.

### Care, trust and ordinary behavior
She brings what was needed, makes a space comfortable and remembers which small inconvenience someone dislikes. Care can mean accepting a hand rather than automatically taking the heavier share. Her tail and body require practical accommodations, not magical immunity to travel hazards.

### Voice and manner
Relaxed, blunt warmth with a dry punchline. She can be quiet without being evasive. Keep the language ordinary and specific; not every feeling needs a highway metaphor.

### Attraction and courtship
Off duty she is earthy, warm and unexpectedly bold with a welcome admirer. She likes straightforward invitations and shared comfort. Her independent private companionship happens separately from freight work, and the script never initiates intimacy while she is driving or substitutes it for rest.

### Everyday texture and preferences
A good stop means ordinary comforts that work: decent food, enough space and a chance to be off duty. She enjoys tools for their usefulness and maps for the possibilities they suggest. Her rented home and hope for a future fleet are personal stakes, not an excuse to make her a secret smuggler. A friend who notices her fatigue can matter more than one impressed by her endurance. She may choose quiet company over another long story about the road.

### History and ordinary life
Rowan grew up around coastal freight depots and eventually built an independent hauling business. Where permitted, she carries mundane supplies through licensed Pelagian trade channels without exposing restricted coordinates or taking clandestine passengers. Years of fixing breakdowns herself taught her to value preparation over bravado.
