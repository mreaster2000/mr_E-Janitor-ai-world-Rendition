# Writing references for the expanded edition

Reviewed 4 November 2026. These are craft references, not sources of character canon. All new dialogue and character-specific development in this edition are original writing. No published character dialogue was copied.

- [Reedsy — 12 Character Archetypes Every Writer Should Know](https://reedsy.com/blog/character-archetypes/): familiar roles can provide recognizable motivations, while individual choices and contradictions prevent a stock figure. This edition uses role combinations as writing aids, never psychological diagnoses or rules tied to nationality.
- [Reedsy — Character Development: How to Write Characters Who Feel Real](https://reedsy.com/blog/character-development/): useful distinctions between what a character wants, what troubles them and what they may need to change. Specific behavior matters more than accumulating interchangeable adjectives.
- [Write for Harlequin — The Roadmap to Writing Romance: 5 Ways to Hone Your Story Idea](https://www.writeforharlequin.com/the-roadmap-to-writing-romance-5-ways-to-hone-your-story-idea/): motivations and internal obstacles give attraction substance. Chemistry should have reasons rooted in the people involved instead of depending on a conveniently arriving external crisis. ## Application to this cast

The combinations listed in the root guide are editorial interpretations of the existing Rendition characters, not claims made by these sources. Each character receives a distinct motive, pressure response, way of giving care, speaking rhythm, courtship style and ordinary-life detail. Four original examples demonstrate those choices. They are possibilities for voice, not events the bot should remember.

Kaia and Aveline remain the reserved Zentreya exceptions. Adult-work occupations already established for other characters remain part of their lives, alongside their other careers. Flirtation can be bold, suggestive and emotionally direct while remaining non-graphic. No archetype overrides another person's choices.

The authoritative source for names, heights, occupations, relationships and world history is this repository at commit `c3ca283b2f9f50d2eeb8a0c4bfb266f4dbda97c4`, especially the current cards, bios and Markdown canon. External superhero continuity is not imported. The pre-existing Zentreya reconstruction notices remain attached to the expanded bios.
