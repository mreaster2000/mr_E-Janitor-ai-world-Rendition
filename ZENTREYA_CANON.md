# Zentreya — Hybrid lineage and recovered cast (November 2026)

> This is a **reconstruction** of the creator's interrupted November 3 expansion, not a verbatim preservation of the inaccessible earlier Word export. Canonical roles below came from recovered user instructions; newly chosen names (except recovered Marisol and Kaia), fine biography details and original-art mappings are editorial proposals awaiting creator corrections.

**Zentreya** denotes a rare lineage of adult women descended from Pelagian people and pre-existing fantasy dragon families. Traditional Pelagian dragons are flying, full-bodied creatures; Zentreya are unusual humanoid hybrid descendants, not one universal biological template. Horns, tails, scales, body shapes, heat tolerance and longevity vary. Do not automatically grant every hybrid flight, fire breath, spellcasting or draconic immortality.

Several generations of Pelagian policy used a selective Zentreya modernization initiative to encourage hybrid daughters to become bridges between an insular island and a changing world. This was a **social role imposed upon or offered to existing people**, not proof that councils manufactured the species in a laboratory. Some entered engineering, civil service and international trade. Others pursued racing, fashion, modeling, nightlife, consensual adult work, freight or simply private happiness. The island's secular government does not worship dragons or godlike residents; its queen and councils still govern.

Pelagia remains **women-only**, culturally diverse, restrictive about outsider entry and technologically unequal after centuries of limited opening. A diaspora exists, including people with American parents and personal lives abroad. Men may be family members or external contacts without gaining automatic residence. Dragon descent does not confer citizenship or unrestricted access by itself. Personal secrets, Management operations and private identities must remain compartmentalized.
